# AI Governance

## Overview

AI Governance is the framework of policies, processes, standards, controls, and oversight that ensures AI systems are developed, deployed, and operated responsibly throughout their lifecycle. It helps organizations manage risk, comply with regulations, protect sensitive data, and ensure AI systems remain trustworthy, transparent, secure, and aligned with business objectives.

Enterprise AI governance combines technical controls with organizational processes to enable responsible and scalable AI adoption.

## Definition

**AI Governance** is the discipline of establishing policies, decision-making processes, accountability, and technical controls for the responsible management of AI systems across their entire lifecycle.

## Objectives

- Understand the purpose of AI governance.
- Learn governance frameworks and responsibilities.
- Explore AI risk management.
- Understand regulatory and compliance requirements.
- Prepare for enterprise-scale AI operations.
