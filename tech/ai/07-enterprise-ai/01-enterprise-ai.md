

# Enterprise AI

## Overview

Enterprise AI is the adoption of Artificial Intelligence across an organization to improve business processes, decision-making, productivity, customer experiences, and innovation. Unlike standalone AI projects, Enterprise AI focuses on building scalable, secure, governed, and production-ready AI capabilities that integrate with existing business systems and workflows.

Successful Enterprise AI combines AI Engineering, MLOps, Platform Engineering, Security, Governance, Observability, and Responsible AI into a unified operating model.

## Definition

**Enterprise AI** is the strategic application of AI technologies, engineering practices, governance frameworks, and operational processes to deliver secure, reliable, scalable, and business-aligned AI solutions across an organization.

- Ignoring security and compliance.
- Lack of monitoring.
