# AI Overview

## Overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating machines and software capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, understanding language, recognizing images, and making decisions.

Today, AI powers many everyday applications such as search engines, chatbots, recommendation systems, voice assistants, autonomous vehicles, and medical diagnosis tools.

## Simple Definition

> **Artificial Intelligence is the ability of a computer system to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence.**

Rather than following only fixed instructions, AI systems can analyze information, identify patterns, and produce useful outputs.

## Why AI Matters

Modern systems generate enormous amounts of data. AI helps process and understand this data faster and more accurately than traditional approaches.

Some major goals of AI are:
- Automate repetitive tasks.
- Assist humans in decision-making.
- Discover patterns hidden in data.
- Improve efficiency and productivity.
- Build intelligent systems that can adapt and learn.

### Traditional Programming

In traditional software development, programmers write explicit rules for every situation.

```text
Input + Program Rules → Output
```

### Artificial Intelligence

Instead of manually defining every rule, AI systems can learn patterns from data.

```text
Input + Data + Learning Algorithm → AI Model → Output
```

## Types of AI

### Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI)

Also called **Weak AI**, ANI is designed for a specific task. This is the type of AI used in the world today.

Examples:
- ChatGPT.
- Google Search ranking.
- Face recognition.
- Netflix recommendations.

### Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)

AGI refers to a theoretical AI system with human-level intelligence that can perform any intellectual task a person can do.

AGI does not currently exist.

### Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI)

ASI is a hypothetical future stage where AI surpasses human intelligence across all domains.

## Challenges and Limitations

Although AI is powerful, it also has limitations.

| Challenge | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Data Dependency | AI requires high-quality data. |
| Bias | Models can inherit biases from training data. |
| Explainability | Complex models may be difficult to understand. |
| Computational Cost | Training advanced models requires significant hardware. |
| Privacy and Security | AI systems often process sensitive information. |

## AI and the Future

AI is becoming a foundational technology similar to the internet or cloud computing. It is expected to continue transforming industries such as healthcare, education, software engineering, finance, and scientific research.

Recent advances in Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs) have accelerated AI adoption across both consumer and enterprise applications.

## Key Takeaways

- Artificial Intelligence enables machines to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence.
- AI includes many areas such as Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP, and Computer Vision.
- Most AI systems today are examples of Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI).
- AI systems are generally built by collecting data, training models, and using those models for inference.
- Modern applications like chatbots, recommendation systems, and image recognition are all powered by AI.
