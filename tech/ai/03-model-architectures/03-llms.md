## Objectives

Large Language Models (LLMs) use Transformer architectures trained on massive datasets to understand and generate human-like text and other forms of content.

Key objectives of LLMs include:
- Understand language patterns from large-scale datasets.
- Generate coherent and context-aware responses.
- Perform tasks such as summarization, translation, reasoning, and coding assistance.
- Support Generative AI applications across multiple domains.
- Provide adaptable foundations for AI-powered products and services.

## Quick Revision

- Large Language Models (LLMs) are AI models trained on massive text and data collections.
- LLMs are built using Transformer architectures.
- Training involves learning billions of parameters from large datasets.
- Inference uses learned parameters to generate responses for user inputs.
- Tokenization converts text into tokens that models can process.
- Prompt engineering improves the quality of model outputs by designing effective inputs.
- LLMs power modern applications such as chatbots, coding assistants, search systems, and enterprise AI solutions.
