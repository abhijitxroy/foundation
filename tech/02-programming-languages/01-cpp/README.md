# Modern C++ - Beginner to AMD SMTS Journey

> Learn Modern C++ from first principles and progressively build the skills required for AMD SMTS (Lead Quality Platform Engineering) interviews.

---

## What is the Goal?

This repository is NOT designed to make you a Modern C++ language expert.

The goal is to become:

- Interview Ready
- Production Ready
- Debugging Oriented
- Testing Oriented
- Platform Engineering Focused
- Comfortable with Modern C++ fundamentals and senior-level discussions

## Learning Approach

Every topic follows the same structure:

1. What is it?
2. Why does it exist?
3. Java comparison (when helpful).
4. Simple examples.
5. Common mistakes.
6. Interview questions.
7. Production examples.
8. Coding exercises.
9. Review and follow-up discussions.
10. Mental models for revision.

We intentionally learn one topic at a time.

---

## Mental Models Over Memorization

Avoid:

```text
57 Topics
 ↓
57 Definitions
```

Prefer:

```text
57 Topics
 ↓
15-20 Mental Models
 ↓
Everything Connected
 ↓
Easy Revision
 ↓
Interview Ready
```

The goal is long-term understanding rather than short-term memorization.

## Senior-Level Thinking Pattern

Always ask:

```text
What problem are we solving?
            ↓
What are the requirements?
            ↓
What are the constraints?
            ↓
What are the trade-offs?
            ↓
Why was this design chosen?
            ↓
How is it used in production?
```

Do not start with the technology.
