# Modern C++ Interview Guide

## Overview

This directory contains interview-focused notes for Modern C++.

Unlike the learning notes, which explain concepts in detail, these documents focus on answering interview questions clearly, confidently, and with a production engineering mindset.

The objective is to bridge the gap between understanding a concept and communicating it effectively during technical interviews.

---

## Learning Approach

For each topic:

1. Study the concept from the corresponding learning notes.
2. Review the interview guide.
3. Practice answering the questions without looking at the answers.
4. Compare your response with the guide.
5. Refine your explanation until it becomes natural and easy to communicate.

---

## Standard Format

Every interview guide follows the same structure.

- Question
- 30-Second Answer
- 2-Minute Answer
- Key Points
- Production Perspective
- Common Mistakes
- Follow-up Questions
- Interviewer's Expectation
- Revision Notes

This makes revision faster and helps build consistent interview communication.

---

## Goal

The goal is not to memorize answers.

The goal is to understand the engineering problem, explain the reasoning behind the solution, discuss trade-offs when appropriate, and communicate ideas with confidence during technical interviews.
