# Modern C++ - Mock Interview Notes

> This file is used to simulate AMD SMTS interview rounds throughout the learning journey.

---

## What is the Goal?

The goal is NOT to memorize answers.

The goal is to learn:

- How to communicate clearly.
- How to explain technical concepts.
- How to discuss trade-offs.
- How to think under pressure.
- How to approach senior-level interview questions.

---

## Answering Pattern

Always try to answer using:

```text
What is it?
     ↓
Why does it exist?
     ↓
How is it used?
     ↓
Production Usage?
     ↓
Trade-offs?
     ↓
Summary
```

### Interview Thinking Framework

Before answering any question, always ask yourself:

```text
WHAT?
 ↓
WHY?
 ↓
HOW?
 ↓
BENEFITS?
 ↓
ENGINEERING IMPACT?
 ↓
BUSINESS IMPACT?
```

Do not rush to syntax or implementation details.

---

## Senior-Level Thinking Pattern

Always think:

```text
Problem
 ↓
Requirements
 ↓
Constraints
 ↓
Trade-offs
 ↓
Technology Selection
 ↓
Production Considerations
 ↓
Maintainability
```

Do not immediately jump to the technology or syntax.

### Three Layer Thinking Model

```text
Technical Impact
      ↓
Engineering Impact
      ↓
Business Impact
```

Examples:

```text
Faster Builds
      ↓
Developer Productivity
      ↓
CI/CD Performance
      ↓
Faster Feedback Loop
      ↓
Release Velocity
      ↓
Infrastructure Cost
      ↓
Engineering Efficiency
```

Senior engineers are expected to discuss more than implementation details whenever appropriate.

## What Will Be Evaluated?

We will evaluate:

- Technical correctness.
- Communication skills.
- Problem-solving ability.
- Production thinking.
- Debugging mindset.
- Modern C++ fundamentals.
- Testing mindset.
- Leadership discussions.

Additional evaluation areas:

- Systems thinking.
- Breadth of thinking.
- Question accuracy.
- Engineering reasoning.
- Ability to justify trade-offs.
- Ability to communicate requirements and constraints.

### Common Improvement Areas

We will continuously improve:

- Correct terminology.
- Answering exactly what was asked.
- Thinking deeply while knowing when to stop.
- Connecting technical discussions with production systems.
