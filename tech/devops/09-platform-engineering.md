


# Platform Engineering

## Overview

As engineering organizations grow, development teams spend increasing amounts of time managing infrastructure, configuring environments, and maintaining delivery pipelines instead of building business features. Platform Engineering addresses this challenge by providing standardized, self-service engineering platforms that improve developer productivity, consistency, and operational excellence.

## Definition

Platform Engineering is the discipline of designing, building, and operating internal platforms that provide reusable infrastructure, automation, and engineering capabilities as self-service products for software development teams.

The goal is to enable developers to focus on delivering business value rather than managing operational complexity.

## Objectives

- Improve developer productivity.
- Standardize engineering workflows.
- Enable self-service infrastructure.
- Reduce operational complexity.
- Increase platform reliability.
- Accelerate software delivery.

## Platform Engineering Model

```text
Platform Team
      │
      ▼
Internal Developer Platform
      │
      ├── Infrastructure
      ├── CI/CD
      ├── Security
      ├── Observability
      ├── Templates
      └── Self-Service APIs
               │
               ▼
Development Teams
```

## Core Concepts

| Concept | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Internal Developer Platform (IDP) | Unified platform for engineering teams. |
| Self-Service | Allow developers to provision resources independently. |
| Golden Paths | Standardized, recommended engineering workflows. |
| Platform APIs | Expose reusable platform capabilities. |
| Developer Experience (DevEx) | Improve engineering productivity and satisfaction. |
| Platform as a Product | Build platforms based on user needs and continuous feedback. |

## Best Practices

- Treat the platform as a product.
- Design for self-service and automation.
- Standardize common engineering workflows.
- Reduce cognitive load for developers.
- Measure platform adoption and developer experience.
- Continuously evolve the platform based on feedback.

## Common Mistakes

- Building a platform without understanding developer needs.
- Over-standardizing every workflow.
- Creating unnecessary platform complexity.
- Ignoring developer experience metrics.
- Treating the platform as an infrastructure project only.

## Interview Focus

Be prepared to explain Platform Engineering, Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs), Golden Paths, self-service infrastructure, developer experience (DevEx), platform APIs, and how Platform Engineering differs from traditional DevOps.
