# Packaging and Testing

> Semiconductor packaging and testing are critical stages in the semiconductor lifecycle. Modern processors are no longer simply manufactured and shipped. They undergo sophisticated packaging, validation, performance optimization, and reliability testing before reaching customers. Advanced packaging technologies have become one of the biggest innovations powering Artificial Intelligence (AI), High Performance Computing (HPC), and next-generation datacenter systems.

## What Is Semiconductor Packaging?

Semiconductor packaging is the process of preparing manufactured chips for deployment into real-world products.

Packaging provides:

- Physical Protection
- Thermal Management
- Electrical Connectivity
- Performance Optimization
- Reliability Improvements
- Multi-Chip Integration

Modern packaging technologies have become critical for:

- Artificial Intelligence
- High Performance Computing
- Cloud Infrastructure
- Datacenters
- Consumer Electronics
- Autonomous Vehicles
