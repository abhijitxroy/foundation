# Semiconductor Business Models

> The semiconductor industry is built upon multiple business models that enable companies to specialize in chip design, manufacturing, software engineering, packaging, testing, and infrastructure. Understanding these business models is essential for understanding how modern semiconductor products are conceived, built, and delivered to customers.

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will understand:

- Major semiconductor business models.
- How semiconductor companies generate revenue.
- Differences between Fabless, IDM, and Foundry companies.
- The semiconductor value chain.
- Industry leaders across various categories.
- Advantages and challenges of different business models.
- How software engineering fits into semiconductor businesses.

## IP Companies

IP (Intellectual Property) companies design processor architectures and license them to semiconductor companies.

### Responsibilities

- CPU Architectures
- DSP Technologies
- AI Architectures
- Processor Designs
- Licensing Technologies

### Revenue Model

```text
            ARM
              |
          Licensing
              |
       -------------------
       |                 |
   Apple             Qualcomm
       |                 |
    Products          Products
       |                 |
     Royalty Payments to ARM
```

### Examples

| Company |
|--------|
| ARM |
| CEVA |
| Rambus |

### Advantages

- High profit margins.
- No manufacturing requirements.
- Highly scalable.

### Challenges

- Heavy R&D investments.
- Architectural leadership requirements.

---

## EDA Companies

EDA stands for Electronic Design Automation.

These companies develop software used for chip design and verification.

### Responsibilities

- Chip Design Tools
- Simulation Platforms
- Verification Tools
- Timing Analysis
- Physical Design Tools

### Revenue Model

```text
           Synopsys
               |
          Software Licensing
               |
         Semiconductor Companies
               |
        Annual Licensing Revenue
```

### Examples

| Company |
|--------|
| Synopsys |
| Cadence |
| Siemens EDA |

### Advantages

- Extremely high entry barriers.
- Large enterprise customers.
- Recurring revenue streams.

### Challenges

- Significant R&D investments.
- Complex software engineering requirements.

---

```text

               |

               |
             GPUs
               |

               |

               |
           Products
               |
```

### Examples

| Company |
|--------|
| NVIDIA |
| AMD |
| Qualcomm |
| Broadcom |
| Apple |
| Google |

- No manufacturing investments.

## Key Takeaways

- Modern semiconductor products are built through collaboration across multiple business models.
- Fabless companies focus on design, while foundries specialize in manufacturing.
- IDMs provide end-to-end semiconductor solutions.
- EDA and IP companies provide foundational technologies for chip development.
- Packaging and testing have become increasingly important because of AI accelerators and advanced computing systems.
- Software engineering is a critical component of semiconductor products and developer ecosystems.
