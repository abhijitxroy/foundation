## Module Completion Criteria

You should be able to confidently explain:

- How semiconductor products are designed.
- How semiconductor products are manufactured.
- How modern AI accelerators operate.
- How semiconductor business models work.
- How enterprise engineering practices influence semiconductor products.
- How developer platforms and compiler ecosystems enable semiconductor adoption.
- How semiconductor organizations build large-scale engineering platforms.
- How to prepare for semiconductor engineering careers and technical interviews.

A learner completing this module should possess both conceptual understanding and system-level engineering knowledge of modern semiconductor technologies.
