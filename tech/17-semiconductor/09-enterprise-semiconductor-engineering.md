# Enterprise Semiconductor Engineering

> Modern semiconductor engineering extends far beyond chip design and manufacturing. Today's semiconductor products require enterprise-scale software engineering practices spanning developer platforms, compiler ecosystems, cloud infrastructure, automation, validation, Artificial Intelligence, security engineering, and release management. Enterprise Semiconductor Engineering brings together hardware, software, and platform engineering disciplines to deliver highly reliable semiconductor products at scale.
