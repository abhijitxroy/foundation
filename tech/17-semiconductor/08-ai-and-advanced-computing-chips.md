# AI and Advanced Computing Chips

> Artificial Intelligence (AI), High Performance Computing (HPC), Cloud Computing, and modern datacenters are driving one of the largest transformations in semiconductor history. Traditional CPUs are no longer sufficient for modern workloads. Advanced computing now relies upon specialized processors including GPUs, TPUs, NPUs, AI accelerators, and heterogeneous computing platforms.
