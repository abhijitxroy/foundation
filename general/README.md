# General

A personal knowledge space for collecting, organizing, and preserving long-term learning beyond engineering. It brings together books, films, language, detective stories, and personal roadmaps into a single, structured knowledge library.

The goal of this section is not to maintain scattered lists, but to build a searchable and continuously evolving collection of ideas, references, and learning resources.

## Structure

```text
                               General
                                  │
         ┌────────────┬───────────┼───────────┬────────────┬────────────┐
         │            │           │           │            │
     Wishlist      Books       Films   Detective Stories  Vocabulary
         │            │           │           │            │
   People, skills  Core       Bengali    Bengali       English
   and subjects    Finance    Finance    International Bengali
   to explore      Growth     Golden     Characters    Phrases
   Reading         Mind       Watchlist
   journey         Society
```

## Sections

| Section | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `wishlist/` | Long-term learning goals, people to know, skills to develop, subjects to explore, reading journey, and travel aspirations. |
| `books/` | A personal knowledge library containing curated book collections, chapter-wise notes, highlights, and revision material. |
| `films/` | Film watchlists and curated movie collections across different themes and interests. |
| `detective-stories/` | Detective, mystery, adventure stories, memorable characters, and related reading collections. |
| `vocabulary/` | A personal vocabulary notebook containing English and Bengali words, phrases, idioms, and language notes. |

## What You Will Find

- Curated reading roadmaps and book notes.
- Personal finance, psychology, philosophy, and society learning resources.
- Film and documentary watchlists.
- Detective stories and memorable fictional characters.
- English and Bengali vocabulary collections.
- Personal learning goals, reading journeys, and wishlists.

## Design Principles

- Keep information simple and easy to revisit.
- Organize content by topic rather than chronology.
- Prefer curated collections over unstructured note dumps.
- Capture important ideas instead of storing raw information.
- Allow wishlist items to gradually evolve into dedicated study notes.
- Keep each topic in its own focused file whenever possible.

## Purpose

- Build and maintain a lifelong personal knowledge base beyond engineering.
- Organize books, films, language, and personal interests in a structured way.
- Preserve important ideas, highlights, and reference material for future use.
- Maintain personal learning roadmaps and curated collections.
- Create a searchable library that is easy to revisit, expand, and learn from over time.
