# Dangerous Books

A curated collection of books that explore propaganda, ideology, mass psychology, power structures, and the formation of political and social narratives. The purpose of this list is to understand different perspectives and improve critical thinking.

## Reading List

- [ ] **The New World Order** — A. Ralph Epperson
- [ ] **The Rape of the Mind** — Joost A. M. Meerloo
- [ ] **Propaganda** — Edward L. Bernays
- [ ] **Notes on Nationalism** — George Orwell
- [ ] **How Democracies Die** — Steven Levitsky & Daniel Ziblatt

## Objective

- Understand how public opinion and mass movements are shaped.
- Learn about propaganda, persuasion, and information control.
- Study the relationship between ideology, institutions, and society.
- Develop the ability to analyze ideas critically from multiple viewpoints.

## Notes

- This collection is intended for learning and critical analysis.
- Inclusion of a book does not imply agreement with its arguments or conclusions.
- When reading, compare ideas across multiple sources and perspectives.
