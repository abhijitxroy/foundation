## Collections

| Collection | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Must Read** | Timeless books that provide enduring value across life, learning, communication, and personal growth. |
| **Finance** | Personal finance, investing, business, entrepreneurship, and wealth creation. |
| **Intelligence** | Learning, psychology, critical thinking, philosophy, and understanding human behavior. |
| **Mental Fitness** | Emotional well-being, resilience, habits, self-awareness, and personal development. |
| **Dangerous Books** | Books that explore propaganda, ideology, power structures, and the shaping of societies and public opinion. |

## Purpose

- Build a personal lifetime reading roadmap.
- Organize books by theme rather than maintaining an unstructured list.
- Explore ideas across engineering, finance, psychology, philosophy, and society.
- Maintain a curated collection of books worth reading and revisiting over time.
