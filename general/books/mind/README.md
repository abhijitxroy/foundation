# Mind

A collection of books on learning, thinking, psychology, philosophy, decision-making, and understanding human behavior. The goal is to develop better judgment, improve critical thinking, and build a deeper understanding of the mind and society.

Each book has its own note containing chapter-wise summaries, important highlights, key ideas, and revision notes.

## Book Notes

Each book in this collection has (or will have) its own note file containing:
- Chapter-wise summaries.
- Main ideas and highlights.
- Important frameworks and mental models.
- Quick revision notes.
- Personal observations and related books.

## Purpose

- Build a better understanding of how people think and behave.
- Develop critical thinking and decision-making skills.
- Learn how knowledge is acquired, organized, and retained.
- Explore psychology, philosophy, and the foundations of human society.
- Maintain a personal knowledge base that can be revisited and expanded over time.
