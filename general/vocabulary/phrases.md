# Phrases

A personal collection of useful English phrases, idioms, and everyday expressions that I want to remember and use naturally in conversation and writing.

## Everyday Expressions

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---------|----------|
| **Long time no see** | A friendly greeting used when meeting someone after a long period. |
| **Don't take it to heart** | Don't be too upset or offended by something. |
| **Did I get you right?** | Asking whether you understood correctly. |
| **It serves you right** | Someone deserved the negative outcome. |
| **What have you been up to?** | Asking what someone has been doing recently. |
| **You rock!** | You did a great job; you are awesome. |
| **It's not worth it.** | The effort or cost is greater than the benefit. |
| **Text me.** | Send me a text message. |
| **There is no room for doubt.** | Something is absolutely certain. |
| **How are you holding up?** | Asking how someone is coping with a difficult situation. |

## Idioms

| Idiom | Meaning |
|--------|----------|
| **Break a leg** | Good luck. |
| **Beat a dead horse** | Waste time discussing or pursuing something that has already been settled. |
| **Eat like a horse** | Eat a very large amount of food. |
| **Hold your horses** | Wait a moment; be patient. |
| **Once in a blue moon** | Very rarely. |
| **Tie the knot** | Get married. |
| **Not rocket science** | Not very difficult. |
| **Under wraps** | Kept secret or confidential. |
