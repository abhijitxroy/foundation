# Bengali

A personal collection of interesting Bengali words, expressions, and culturally meaningful terms that I want to remember and use naturally.

## Words & Expressions

| Word / Phrase | Meaning / Note |
|--------------|----------------|
| **আকাশকুসুম (Akashkusum)** | An unrealistic dream or an impossible ambition; equivalent to the English expression *"castles in the air."* |

## Purpose

- Preserve beautiful and meaningful Bengali words.
- Learn the cultural and literary context behind expressions.
- Build a personal reference for uncommon or memorable Bengali vocabulary.
