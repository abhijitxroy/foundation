# Golden Films

A curated collection of timeless films that are widely regarded for their storytelling, artistic achievement, cultural impact, and lasting influence. These are films worth watching at least once.

## World Cinema Classics

- [ ] *Bicycle Thieves* (1948) — Italian neorealist classic.
- [ ] *Lawrence of Arabia* (1962) — Epic historical adventure.
- [ ] *High and Low* (1963) — Crime drama directed by Akira Kurosawa.
- [ ] *Fargo* (1996) — Crime thriller by the Coen Brothers.
- [ ] *Pulp Fiction* (1994) — Landmark crime film by Quentin Tarantino.
- [ ] *Talk to Her* (2002) — Drama directed by Pedro Almodóvar.

## Purpose

- Explore films that have shaped the history of world cinema.
- Appreciate exceptional storytelling, direction, and cinematography.
- Build a personal collection of timeless and influential movies.
- Maintain a curated watchlist of films worth revisiting throughout life.
