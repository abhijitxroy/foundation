# Finance

A curated collection of films, documentaries, and series related to finance, investing, business, entrepreneurship, and economic history. The goal is to better understand markets, human behavior, and the financial world through storytelling.

## Finance & Investing

- [ ] *The Big Short* (2015) — The 2008 global financial crisis.
- [ ] *Margin Call* (2011) — The beginning of a market collapse.
- [ ] *Boiler Room* (2000) — Sales, greed, and stock market fraud.
- [ ] *The Wolf of Wall Street* (2013) — Excess, ambition, and financial crime.
- [ ] *The Laundromat* (2019) — Offshore finance and the Panama Papers.

## Business & Entrepreneurship

- [ ] *Undercover Billionaire* — Building a business from scratch.
- [ ] *The Social Network* (2010) — The creation of Facebook and startup culture.
- [ ] *The Founder* (2016) — The rise of the McDonald's empire.

## Markets & Scams

- [ ] *Gafla* (2006) — Inspired by the Harshad Mehta stock market scam.
- [ ] *Scam 1992: The Harshad Mehta Story* (2020) — The story behind one of India's biggest financial scandals.

## Series

- [ ] *Billions* — Hedge funds, finance, and power.

## Purpose

- Understand the basics of finance and investing through real-world stories.
- Learn about market psychology, bubbles, and financial crises.
- Explore entrepreneurship, business building, and wealth creation.
- Maintain a personal watchlist of finance and business related films and series.
