# Figures to Know

A curated collection of notable personalities whose lives, achievements, and contributions I want to explore and understand.

## Bengali Icons

- [ ] **Suresh Biswas** — Ringmaster, adventurer, doctor, and soldier.
- [ ] **Radhanath Sikdar (1813–1870)** — Mathematician, surveyor, and the first person to calculate the height of Mount Everest.
- [ ] **Bholanath Bikramaditya Sikdar** — 19th-century Bengali traveler and writer.

## Bengali Scientists

- [ ] **Meghnad Saha** — Astrophysicist; mentored by Prafulla Chandra Ray.
- [ ] **Jagadish Chandra Bose** — Pioneer of radio science and plant physiology; mentor of Satyendra Nath Bose.
- [ ] **Prafulla Chandra Ray** — Chemist, educator, entrepreneur, and founder of Bengal Chemicals.
- [ ] **Satyendra Nath Bose** — Theoretical physicist who collaborated with Albert Einstein.
- [ ] **Ashoke Sen** — Theoretical physicist known for major contributions to string theory.
- [ ] **Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar** — Chemist and scientific administrator; mentored by Prafulla Chandra Ray.
