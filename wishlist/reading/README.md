# Reading

This section focuses on improving my reading habits, learning techniques, and knowledge acquisition through carefully selected books and reading methodologies.

## Topics

| Topic | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Books on Reading | Books that improve reading techniques and comprehension. |
| Books on Learning | Books about effective learning, memory, and note-taking. |
| Books on Productivity | Books that improve focus, productivity, and personal effectiveness. |
| Books on Finance | Books that build financial knowledge and investing skills. |
| Reading Principles | Best practices, reading workflows, and lifelong reading habits. |

## Goals

- [ ] Become a better reader.
- [ ] Build a lifelong reading habit.
- [ ] Improve comprehension, retention, and critical thinking.
- [ ] Read with purpose rather than simply finishing books.

## Reading Principles

- Read consistently rather than occasionally.
- Take notes and summarize key ideas.
- Revisit books that significantly change your thinking.
- Prioritize quality over quantity.
