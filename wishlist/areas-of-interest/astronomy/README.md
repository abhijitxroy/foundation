# Astronomy

Astronomy is the study of celestial objects, space, and the universe. This section serves as a roadmap for understanding astronomical concepts, from our Solar System to the large-scale structure of the cosmos.

## Topics

| Topic | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Solar System | Planets, moons, asteroids, comets, and other objects orbiting the Sun. |
| Stars | Stellar formation, lifecycle, classification, and evolution. |
| Constellations | Recognizable star patterns and their significance. |
| Galaxies | Types, structure, and evolution of galaxies. |
| Black Holes | Formation, properties, and role in the universe. |
| Telescopes | Optical, radio, and space telescopes used for astronomical observations. |
