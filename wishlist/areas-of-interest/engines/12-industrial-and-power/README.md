# Industrial and Power Systems

A study roadmap for engines, turbines, motors, and power-conversion systems used to generate electricity, provide mechanical power, and operate industrial, agricultural, and construction machinery.

## Core Topics

| Topic | What to Understand |
|------|---------------------|
| Generator Engines | Engines coupled to generators to convert fuel energy into electrical power. |
| Industrial Gas Turbines | Gas turbines used for electricity generation and large mechanical drives. |
| Steam Turbines | Steam-driven turbines used for power generation and industrial processes. |
| Microturbines | Small gas-turbine systems used for distributed power generation and combined heat and power. |
| Combined-Cycle Power | Gas turbines combined with steam turbines to improve overall power-generation efficiency. |
| Agricultural Engines | Engines used in tractors, harvesters, pumps, and other agricultural machinery. |
| Construction Equipment Engines | Engines used in excavators, bulldozers, loaders, cranes, compressors, and other heavy equipment. |
