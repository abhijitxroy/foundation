# Electric Motors

A study roadmap for electric motors and electric drive systems that convert electrical energy into mechanical power.

## Core Topics

| Topic | What to Understand |
|------|---------------------|
| Motor Basics | How electrical energy creates electromagnetic force and rotational motion. |
| DC Motors | Construction, commutation, operation, speed control, torque, and applications. |
| AC Motors | Alternating-current motor principles, construction, operation, and applications. |
| Induction Motors | Rotating magnetic fields, induction, slip, rotor types, torque production, and applications. |
| Permanent-Magnet Motors | Permanent magnets, electromagnetic torque, efficiency, construction, and applications. |
| Brushless Motors | Electronic commutation, rotor position sensing, controllers, torque, and applications. |
| Motor Controllers | Inverters, switching, control methods, speed control, torque control, and power electronics. |

## Study Progression

1. Understand electromagnetism and motor fundamentals.
2. Learn basic DC motors.
3. Study AC motors.
4. Understand induction motors.
5. Study synchronous and permanent-magnet motors.
6. Learn brushless motor operation.
7. Study motor controllers and inverters.
8. Understand motor performance and efficiency.
9. Learn thermal management.
10. Compare different motor technologies.
11. Explore applications across vehicles, industry, robotics, and other machines.

## Long-Term Goal

Be able to look at any electric motor and understand:

- How electrical energy becomes mechanical motion.
- How electromagnetic torque is generated.
- How the rotor and stator interact.
- How speed and torque are controlled.
- Why different motor architectures exist.
- How the motor is cooled.
- How the controller and power electronics affect performance.
- Why a particular motor is selected for a particular application.
