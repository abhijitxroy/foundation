# Engine & Propulsion Fundamentals

The common foundation for understanding engines, motors, turbines, and propulsion systems.

These fundamentals should be understood before studying specialized propulsion technologies.

## Core Topics

| Topic | What to Understand |
|------|---------------------|
| Energy | Forms of energy, energy conversion, conservation of energy, and energy flow. |
| Power | Mechanical and electrical power, power output, power density, and the relationship between energy and time. |
| Torque | Rotational force, torque production, torque curves, and the relationship between torque and power. |
| Thrust | How propulsion systems produce thrust and the difference between thrust and mechanical power. |
| Efficiency | Thermal, mechanical, electrical, propulsive, and overall system efficiency. |
| Thermodynamics | Temperature, pressure, heat, work, thermodynamic cycles, entropy, and energy conversion. |
| Combustion | Fuel, oxidizer, ignition, combustion chemistry, flame, heat release, emissions, and combustion efficiency. |
| Heat Transfer | Conduction, convection, radiation, cooling, heat exchangers, and thermal management. |
| Propulsion Basics | How engines, motors, turbines, and propulsion systems convert energy into useful work or thrust. |

## Important Concepts

### Energy

Understand how different energy sources are converted into useful forms of energy.

Examples include:

- Chemical energy
- Electrical energy
- Thermal energy
- Nuclear energy
- Mechanical energy

### Power

Understand how quickly energy is transferred or converted.

Important relationships include:

- Power
- Torque
- Rotational speed
- Mechanical work

### Torque

Understand rotational force and why torque is important for machines such as:

- Cars
- Motorcycles
- Trucks
- Ships
- Industrial machinery

### Thrust

Understand how a propulsion system generates a force that moves a vehicle through:

- Air
- Water
- Space

### Efficiency

Understand why no real engine or propulsion system converts all available energy into useful output.

Compare:

- Thermal efficiency
- Mechanical efficiency
- Electrical efficiency
- Propulsive efficiency
- Overall efficiency

## Thermodynamic Cycles

Build an understanding of the major cycles used by different engines and power systems:

- Otto cycle
- Diesel cycle
- Dual cycle
- Brayton cycle
- Rankine cycle
- Stirling cycle
- Carnot cycle

## Propulsion Concepts

Understand the difference between:

- Engine
- Motor
- Turbine
- Powerplant
- Powertrain
- Propulsion system
- Thruster
- Rocket engine

Also understand the difference between:

- Power
- Torque
- Thrust
- Speed
- Velocity
- Energy

## Long-Term Goal

Develop enough fundamental understanding to look at any engine or propulsion system and answer:

- What is its energy source?
- How is that energy converted?
- How is power or thrust produced?
- What is the working cycle?
- What are the major components?
- How efficient is it?
- What limits its performance?
- Why is this technology suitable for its application?
