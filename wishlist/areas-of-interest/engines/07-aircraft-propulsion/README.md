# Aircraft Propulsion

A study roadmap for propulsion systems used to move aircraft through the atmosphere, covering piston engines, gas turbines, high-speed air-breathing engines, and electric propulsion.

## Core Topics

| Topic | What to Understand |
|------|---------------------|
| Piston Aircraft Engines | Reciprocating engines used in light aircraft, including their construction, operation, cooling, ignition, and performance. |
| Turboprop Engines | Gas turbines that use a large portion of their power to drive a propeller. |
| Turbofan Engines | Gas turbines that use a fan and bypass airflow to produce efficient aircraft thrust. |
| Turbojet Engines | Gas turbines that produce thrust primarily through high-velocity exhaust. |
| Turboshaft Engines | Gas turbines designed primarily to deliver shaft power, especially for helicopters and other machinery. |
| Ramjet Engines | Air-breathing engines that use the vehicle's forward motion to compress incoming air. |
| Scramjet Engines | Supersonic-combustion ramjets designed for very high-speed flight. |
| Pulsejet Engines | Simple intermittent-combustion jet engines that produce thrust through repeated combustion cycles. |
| Electric Aircraft Propulsion | Electric motors, batteries, power electronics, and hybrid systems used for aircraft propulsion. |

## Study Progression

1. Understand how aircraft generate thrust.
2. Learn piston aircraft engines.
3. Understand propellers and propeller efficiency.
4. Study the basic gas-turbine cycle.
5. Learn turboprops.
6. Study turbojets.
7. Study turbofans.
8. Learn turboshaft engines.
9. Study ramjets and pulsejets.
10. Understand scramjets and hypersonic propulsion.
11. Study electric and hybrid-electric aircraft propulsion.
12. Compare propulsion systems across different aircraft.

## Long-Term Goal

Be able to look at an aircraft and understand:

- What propulsion system it uses.
- How air and fuel move through the engine.
- How energy is converted into thrust or shaft power.
- Why a propeller, turboprop, turbofan, turbojet, turboshaft, ramjet, or scramjet is appropriate for a particular application.
- How speed, altitude, efficiency, temperature, and aircraft mission affect propulsion-system selection.
- How emerging electric, hybrid, hydrogen, and advanced propulsion technologies may change aviation.
