# Marine Propulsion

A study roadmap for propulsion systems used to move ships, boats, submarines, ferries, and other watercraft.

## Core Topics

| Topic | What to Understand |
|------|---------------------|
| Marine Diesel Engines | Large reciprocating diesel engines used for propulsion and onboard power generation. |
| Gas Turbine Propulsion | Gas turbines used to produce shaft power for high-speed marine applications. |
| Steam Turbine Propulsion | Steam turbines driven by high-pressure steam for marine propulsion and power generation. |
| Diesel-Electric Propulsion | Diesel engines driving generators that supply electric motors connected to the propeller system. |
| Nuclear Propulsion | Nuclear reactors supplying heat and power for propulsion, particularly in large military vessels and submarines. |
| Electric Propulsion | Electric motors powered by batteries, generators, or other electrical sources. |
| Hybrid Propulsion | Combined mechanical and electric propulsion systems designed to improve flexibility and efficiency. |
| Alternative Fuels | Marine propulsion using fuels such as LNG, methanol, ammonia, hydrogen, and other alternative fuels. |

## Study Progression

1. Understand how ships generate propulsion.
2. Learn marine diesel engines.
3. Understand propellers and shaft systems.
4. Study gas-turbine propulsion.
5. Study steam-turbine propulsion.
6. Learn diesel-electric propulsion.
7. Study electric propulsion.
8. Understand hybrid propulsion.
9. Study nuclear propulsion.
10. Explore alternative marine fuels.
11. Compare propulsion systems across different vessel types.
12. Understand why a particular propulsion architecture is selected for a particular vessel.

## Long-Term Goal

Be able to look at a vessel and understand:

- What propulsion system it uses.
- Where its energy comes from.
- How that energy becomes shaft power or thrust.
- How the engine, gearbox, motor, shaft, and propeller work together.
- Why different vessels use different propulsion systems.
- How vessel size, speed, range, operating profile, and mission influence propulsion-system selection.
- How electric, hybrid, nuclear, and alternative-fuel technologies are changing marine propulsion.
