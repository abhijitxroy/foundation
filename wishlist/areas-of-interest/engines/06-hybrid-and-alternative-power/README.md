# Hybrid and Alternative Power

A study roadmap for propulsion and power systems that combine multiple energy-conversion technologies or use fuels and energy sources beyond conventional petrol and diesel.

## Core Topics

| Topic | What to Understand |
|------|---------------------|
| Hybrid Powertrains | How internal-combustion engines, electric motors, batteries, and control systems work together. |
| Plug-in Hybrids | Hybrid vehicles with externally rechargeable batteries and multiple operating modes. |
| Hydrogen Engines | Internal-combustion engines designed to use hydrogen as a fuel. |
| Fuel Cell Systems | How fuel cells convert chemical energy directly into electrical energy for electric propulsion. |
| Battery-Electric Systems | Batteries, inverters, electric motors, and supporting systems used for propulsion. |
| Alternative Fuels | Fuels such as CNG, LNG, LPG, ethanol, methanol, biofuels, hydrogen, ammonia, and synthetic fuels. |

## Study Progression

1. Understand why alternative power systems are being developed.
2. Learn basic hybrid powertrain architectures.
3. Study battery-electric systems.
4. Understand fuel-cell systems.
5. Study hydrogen internal-combustion engines.
6. Learn about alternative fuels.
7. Understand energy storage.
8. Compare efficiency, performance, cost, range, and infrastructure.
9. Explore applications across land, marine, aviation, and industrial systems.
10. Study emerging low-carbon propulsion technologies.

## Long-Term Goal

Be able to explain:

- How different hybrid architectures work.
- How batteries and electric motors complement combustion engines.
- How hydrogen can be used in both engines and fuel cells.
- How fuel cells differ from combustion engines.
- Why different alternative fuels require different storage and infrastructure.
- Why a particular energy source and power-conversion system is suitable for a particular application.
