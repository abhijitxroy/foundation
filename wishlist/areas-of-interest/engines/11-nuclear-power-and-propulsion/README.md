# Nuclear Power and Propulsion

A study roadmap for systems that use nuclear energy as a source of heat or electrical power for propulsion and other high-energy applications.

This area connects nuclear reactors with steam turbines, electrical power systems, marine propulsion, and advanced space propulsion.

## Core Topics

| Topic | What to Understand |
|------|---------------------|
| Nuclear Reactors | How nuclear fission produces heat and how reactors control and transfer that heat. |
| Nuclear Steam Turbines | How reactor heat produces steam that drives turbines and generates mechanical or electrical power. |
| Nuclear Marine Propulsion | How reactor-generated heat is converted into propulsion power for ships and submarines. |
| Nuclear Thermal Propulsion | How a nuclear reactor directly heats a propellant to produce high-velocity exhaust. |
| Nuclear Electric Propulsion | How nuclear energy is converted into electricity that powers electric propulsion systems. |
