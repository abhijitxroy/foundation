# Must Visit World Tour

A curated collection of the world's most remarkable travel destinations.

## Selection Criteria

- Lifetime-worthy experiences
- Safe and practical for tourism
- Unique landscapes or culture
- High scenic or historical value
- Worth the travel time and cost
