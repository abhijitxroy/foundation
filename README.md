# Foundations

A personal knowledge repository for building, organizing, and preserving long-term understanding across engineering, technology, books, films, language, and lifelong learning.

The goal of this repository is not to collect random notes, but to create a structured, searchable, and continuously evolving foundation of knowledge that can be revisited and expanded over time.

## Structure

```text
                                  Foundations
                                        │
               ┌────────────────────────┼────────────────────────┐
               │                        │                        │
         Engineering                General                 Repository
               │                        │                        │
     ┌─────────┼─────────┐     ┌────────┼────────┐        ┌──────┴──────┐
     │         │         │     │        │        │        │             │
 Computing     AI     Core   Books   Films  Vocabulary  Diagrams    Glossary
 Concepts            Topics     │
                                ├── Core
                                ├── Finance
                                ├── Growth
                                ├── Mind
                                ├── Society
                                └── Book Notes
```

## Sections

| Section | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **engineering/** | Foundational engineering and computer science concepts, including AI, cloud, databases, networking, operating systems, security, software architecture, and related topics. |
| **general/** | Curated collections, books, films, vocabulary, detective stories, personal learning roadmaps, and other non-engineering knowledge. |
| **diagrams/** | Visual learning resources, architecture diagrams, concept maps, and illustrations used across the repository. |
| **glossary/** | Repository-wide terminology, abbreviations, and quick-reference definitions. |

## What You Will Find

- Engineering and computer science fundamentals.
- AI and modern technology concepts.
- Curated book collections with chapter-wise notes and highlights.
- Personal finance, investing, and business learning resources.
- Psychology, philosophy, and critical thinking materials.
- Films, vocabulary, detective stories, and general learning collections.
- Diagrams, concept maps, and quick-reference notes.

## Design Principles

- Build knowledge from first principles.
- Keep explanations simple, practical, and easy to revisit.
- Organize information by topic rather than chronology.
- Prefer curated collections over scattered notes.
- Capture important ideas instead of storing raw information.
- Treat the repository as a lifelong learning system.

## Who Is This For?

- Students building strong fundamentals.
- Engineers who want to strengthen and revisit core concepts.
- Self-learners exploring technology and the world around them.
- Readers who enjoy building a personal knowledge library.
- Anyone who values organized, long-term knowledge collection.

## Purpose

- Build strong engineering and general knowledge foundations.
- Preserve the most valuable ideas from books and other learning resources.
- Maintain curated collections of books, films, and personal learning roadmaps.
- Organize personal interests and long-term learning goals.
- Create a searchable reference for continuous learning and quick revision.
- Develop a personal knowledge base that can grow over a lifetime.
