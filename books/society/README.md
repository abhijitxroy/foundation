# Society

A collection of books on society, politics, ideology, propaganda, institutions, and the forces that shape public opinion. The goal is to understand how societies function, how narratives are built, and how power influences people and institutions.

Each book has its own note containing chapter-wise summaries, important highlights, key ideas, and revision notes.

## Book Notes

Each book in this collection has (or will have) its own note file containing:
- Chapter-wise summaries.
- Main ideas and highlights.
- Important frameworks and mental models.
- Quick revision notes.
- Personal observations and related books.

## Purpose

- Understand how public opinion and mass movements are shaped.
- Learn about propaganda, persuasion, and information control.
- Explore the relationship between ideology, institutions, and society.
- Study different political and philosophical viewpoints.
- Develop the ability to analyze ideas critically from multiple perspectives.
- Maintain a personal knowledge base that can be revisited and expanded over time.

## Notes

- This collection is intended for learning, historical understanding, and critical analysis.
- Inclusion of a book does not imply agreement with its arguments or conclusions.
- Compare ideas across multiple sources and viewpoints rather than relying on a single perspective.
- Focus on understanding how ideas influence society, institutions, and human behavior.
