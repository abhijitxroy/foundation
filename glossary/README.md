# Glossary

A centralized reference for important terms, abbreviations, and definitions used throughout the repository. The glossary helps keep concepts consistent across engineering and general topics, making notes easier to understand and quicker to revisit.

Rather than repeatedly explaining the same ideas, this section acts as a shared vocabulary for the entire knowledge base.

## Structure

```text
                               Glossary
                                   │
               ┌───────────────────┼───────────────────┐
               │                   │                   │
         Engineering           General          Abbreviations
               │                   │                   │
     Computing, AI, OS,    History, Politics,     Acronyms,
     Networking, Cloud,    Economics, Science,    Short Forms,
     Security, etc.        Geography, etc.        Technical Terms
```

## Collections

| Collection | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Engineering** | Technical terms and concepts related to computing, operating systems, networking, AI, cloud computing, databases, security, and software architecture. |
| **General** | Common terms from history, politics, economics, philosophy, science, geography, books, and other non-engineering subjects. |
| **Abbreviations** | Frequently used acronyms, initialisms, and short forms across all domains covered by the repository. |

## What You Will Find

- Short and easy-to-understand definitions.
- Engineering and computer science terminology.
- AI and modern technology vocabulary.
- General knowledge and interdisciplinary concepts.
- Frequently used abbreviations and acronyms.
- Quick-reference explanations linked to topics across the repository.

## Design Principles

- Keep definitions concise and practical.
- Prefer simple language over textbook-style explanations.
- Avoid duplicate definitions across multiple notes.
- Maintain a single source of truth for commonly used terms.
- Make information easy to search and quick to revise.

## Purpose

- Maintain a centralized knowledge dictionary for the repository.
- Make notes easier to understand without repeatedly redefining concepts.
- Build a searchable reference for quick revision and long-term learning.
- Keep terminology consistent across engineering and general domains.
- Create a glossary that can grow alongside the rest of the knowledge base.
