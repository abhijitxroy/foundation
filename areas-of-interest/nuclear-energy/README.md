# Nuclear Energy

Nuclear energy is energy stored in atomic nuclei. It can be released through nuclear reactions, primarily **fission** and **fusion**.

## Scope

This section covers:

- Nuclear physics
- Nuclear reactions
- Nuclear reactors
- Nuclear fuel cycles
- Thorium
- Nuclear safety
- Nuclear economics
- Nuclear fusion
