# Nuclear Economics

Nuclear economics examines the cost, investment, financing, operation, fuel, and long-term value of nuclear power.

## Scope

- Capital cost
- Operating cost
- Levelized cost of electricity
- Fuel cost
- Nuclear vs other energy sources
