# Nuclear vs Other Energy

Different energy sources have different cost structures, operating characteristics, resource requirements, and environmental impacts.

## Basic Comparison

| Source | Main Energy Conversion | Dispatchability | Fuel Cost | Major Cost Driver |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nuclear | Fission → Heat → Electricity | High | Relatively low | Capital + financing |
| Coal | Combustion → Heat → Electricity | High | Significant | Fuel + plant |
| Natural Gas | Combustion → Heat → Electricity | High | Significant | Fuel |
| Solar | Sunlight → Electricity | Variable | None | Capital + storage/grid |
| Wind | Wind → Electricity | Variable | None | Capital + grid |
| Hydro | Water → Electricity | High, site-dependent | Very low | Capital + site |
