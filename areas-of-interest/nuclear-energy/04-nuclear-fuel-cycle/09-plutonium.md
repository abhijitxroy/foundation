# Plutonium

Plutonium is a heavy radioactive element with atomic number 94.

## Important Isotopes

- Pu-239 — fissile
- Pu-240
- Pu-241
- Pu-242

## Pu-239 Production

Pu-239 is produced from U-238 through neutron capture and radioactive decay.

U-238
  ↓ neutron capture
U-239
  ↓ beta decay
Np-239
