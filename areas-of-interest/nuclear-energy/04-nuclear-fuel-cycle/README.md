# Nuclear Fuel Cycle

The nuclear fuel cycle covers the complete lifecycle of nuclear fuel, from resource extraction to reactor use, recycling, and waste management.

## Scope

- Uranium mining
- Uranium processing
- Uranium enrichment
- Fuel fabrication
- Reactor fuel
- Spent fuel
- Reprocessing
- Plutonium
- Closed fuel cycle
- Open vs closed fuel cycle
