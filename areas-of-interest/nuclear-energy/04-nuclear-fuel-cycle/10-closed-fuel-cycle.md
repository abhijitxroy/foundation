# Closed Fuel Cycle

A closed nuclear fuel cycle recovers and reuses useful nuclear materials from spent fuel instead of treating all spent fuel as waste.

## Basic Cycle

```text
Nuclear Resource
      ↓
Fuel Fabrication
      ↓
Reactor
      ↓
Spent Fuel
      ↓
Reprocessing
      ↓
Recovered Nuclear Materials
      ↓
New Fuel
      ↓
Reactor
