# Reactor Fuel

Reactor fuel is the nuclear material loaded into a reactor to sustain controlled nuclear reactions and produce energy.

## Main Requirements

Reactor fuel must:

- Contain suitable fissile material
- Withstand high temperature
- Withstand intense radiation
- Maintain structural integrity
- Transfer heat efficiently
- Remain compatible with coolant and reactor materials

## Common Fuel Materials

Examples include:

- Uranium
- Uranium dioxide (UO₂)
- Mixed oxide (MOX)
- Uranium-plutonium fuels
- Thorium-based fuels

## Fuel Forms

Depending on reactor design, fuel may be provided as:

- Pellets
- Rods
- Bundles
- Plates
- Liquid fuel in some advanced designs
