# Spent Fuel

Spent fuel is nuclear fuel that has been removed from a reactor after being used for a period of time.

It is no longer normally suitable for continued operation in its original reactor position, but it still contains radioactive and potentially useful materials.

## What Spent Fuel Contains

```text
Spent Fuel
├── Unused Uranium
├── Fission Products
├── Plutonium
