# Nuclear Fuel Cycle

The nuclear fuel cycle is the complete sequence of activities involved in producing, using, processing, and managing nuclear fuel.

## Basic Cycle

```text
Uranium / Thorium Resources
          ↓
Mining
          ↓
Processing
          ↓
Fuel Preparation
          ↓
Reactor
          ↓
Spent Fuel
          ↓
Storage / Reprocessing
          ↓
Waste / Recovered Material
