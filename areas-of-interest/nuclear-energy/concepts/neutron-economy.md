# Neutron Economy

Neutron economy describes how effectively a nuclear reactor uses the neutrons produced by fission.

## Basic Principle

```text
Fission
  ↓
Neutrons Produced
  ↓
┌───────────────┬────────────────┐
│Useful Neutrons│ Lost Neutrons  │
│ More fission  │ Leakage        │
│ Breeding      │ Absorption     │
└───────────────┴────────────────┘
