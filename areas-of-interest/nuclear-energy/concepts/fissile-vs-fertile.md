# Fissile vs Fertile

Fissile and fertile describe two different roles that nuclear materials can play in a reactor fuel cycle.

## Fissile Material

A fissile material can sustain a nuclear fission chain reaction after absorbing suitable neutrons.

Examples:

- U-235
- Pu-239
- U-233
