# Fast Breeder Reactor

A Fast Breeder Reactor (FBR) is a fast reactor designed to produce energy while converting fertile material into additional fissile material.

## Basic Principle

```text
Fissile Fuel
    ↓
Fission
    ↓
Energy + Fast Neutrons
    ↓
Fertile Material
    ↓
Neutron Capture + Decay
    ↓
New Fissile Material
