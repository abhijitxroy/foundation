# Control Rods

Control rods are neutron-absorbing components used to control the rate of a nuclear chain reaction.

## Basic Function

```text
Control Rods inserted
        ↓
More neutrons absorbed
        ↓
Lower reactivity
        ↓
Lower reactor power

Control Rods withdrawn
        ↓
Fewer neutrons absorbed
        ↓
Higher reactivity
        ↓
Higher reactor power
