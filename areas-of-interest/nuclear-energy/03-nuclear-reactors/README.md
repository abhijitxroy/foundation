# Nuclear Reactors

A nuclear reactor is a system designed to control nuclear reactions and safely convert nuclear energy into useful heat or other forms of energy.

## Scope

- Reactor fundamentals
- Reactor core
- Moderator
- Coolant
- Control rods
- Heat removal
- Steam cycle
- PWR
- BWR
- PHWR
- Fast reactors
- Fast breeder reactors
- Molten salt reactors
- Small modular reactors
