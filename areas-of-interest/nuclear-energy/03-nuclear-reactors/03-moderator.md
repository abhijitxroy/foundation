# Moderator

A moderator is a material used in some nuclear reactors to slow down fast neutrons.

## Why Slow Neutrons?

Fission probability depends on neutron energy.

In reactors such as PWRs and PHWRs, slower **thermal neutrons** are more effective at causing fission in U-235.

```text
Fast neutron
     ↓
Collisions with moderator
     ↓
Slower neutron
     ↓
Thermal neutron
     ↓
Higher probability of U-235 fission
