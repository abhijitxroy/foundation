# Boiling Water Reactor (BWR)

A Boiling Water Reactor (BWR) uses ordinary water as both coolant and moderator. Water boils directly inside the reactor vessel to produce steam.

## Basic Principle

```text
Reactor Core
     ↓
Water boils
     ↓
Steam
     ↓
Turbine
     ↓
Generator
     ↓
Electricity
