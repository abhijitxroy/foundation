# Pressurized Water Reactor (PWR)

A Pressurized Water Reactor (PWR) uses ordinary water as both coolant and moderator.

## Basic Principle

Water in the primary loop is kept at high pressure so it does not boil inside the reactor core.

```text
Reactor Core
     ↓
Hot pressurized water
     ↓
Steam Generator
     ↓
Steam
     ↓
Turbine
     ↓
Generator
     ↓
Electricity
