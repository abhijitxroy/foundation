# Pressurized Heavy Water Reactor (PHWR)

A Pressurized Heavy Water Reactor (PHWR) uses heavy water (`D₂O`) as the moderator and coolant.

PHWR technology is central to India's nuclear power programme.

## Basic Principle

```text
Uranium Fuel
     ↓
Nuclear Fission
     ↓
Heat + Neutrons
     ↓
Heavy Water Coolant
     ↓
Steam Generator
     ↓
Steam
     ↓
Turbine
     ↓
Generator
     ↓
Electricity
