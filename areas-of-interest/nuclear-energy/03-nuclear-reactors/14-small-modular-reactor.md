# Small Modular Reactor

A Small Modular Reactor (SMR) is a nuclear reactor designed with a smaller power output and modular construction compared with large conventional reactors.

## Core Idea

```text
Conventional Nuclear Plant
        ↓
Large Reactor
        ↓
Large Site + Infrastructure

SMR
        ↓
Smaller Reactor Modules
        ↓
Factory / Modular Construction
        ↓
Multiple Modules if Required
