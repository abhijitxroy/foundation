# Neutron Physics

Neutrons are electrically neutral particles found in atomic nuclei. Free neutrons are also central to nuclear reactions.

## Basic Properties

- Charge: **0**
- Mass: approximately 1 atomic mass unit
- Location: nucleus when bound
- Can exist freely outside the nucleus

## Why Neutrons Matter

Because neutrons have no electric charge, they can enter an atomic nucleus without being repelled by the positively charged protons.

This makes them extremely important in nuclear reactions.
