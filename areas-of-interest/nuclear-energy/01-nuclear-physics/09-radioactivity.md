# Radioactivity

Radioactivity is the spontaneous transformation of an unstable atomic nucleus into a more stable state.

The unstable nucleus is called a **radioactive nucleus** or **radionuclide**.

## Why Radioactive Decay Happens

A nucleus can be unstable because of its:

- Proton-to-neutron ratio
- Large nuclear size
- Internal energy state

The nucleus changes by emitting radiation or particles.

```text
Unstable nucleus
       ↓
Radioactive decay
       ↓
New nucleus + radiation
