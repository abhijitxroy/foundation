# Nuclear Binding Energy

Nuclear binding energy is the energy required to completely separate a nucleus into its individual protons and neutrons.

It also represents the energy released when those nucleons form a bound nucleus.

## Why Nuclei Stay Together

Protons repel each other electrically, but the **strong nuclear force** binds protons and neutrons together at very short distances.

Protons + Neutrons
        ↓
 Strong nuclear force
