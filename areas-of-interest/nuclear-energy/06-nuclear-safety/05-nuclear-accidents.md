# Nuclear Accidents

A nuclear accident is an unplanned event involving a nuclear facility that can compromise safety and potentially cause harm to people or the environment.

## Why Accidents Matter

Nuclear accidents are studied to understand:

- Failure mechanisms
- Safety-system performance
- Human factors
- Emergency response
- Design weaknesses
- Lessons for future reactor designs

## Major Accident Sequences

A severe reactor accident can involve multiple failures.
