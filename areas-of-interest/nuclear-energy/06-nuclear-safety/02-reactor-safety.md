# Reactor Safety

Reactor safety is the set of systems, principles, and practices used to prevent accidents, control abnormal conditions, and protect people and the environment.

## Safety Objectives

A nuclear reactor must:

- Control the nuclear chain reaction
- Remove heat from the core
- Prevent radioactive material release
- Maintain containment
- Safely shut down during abnormal conditions
