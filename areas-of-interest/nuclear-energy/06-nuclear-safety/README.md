# Nuclear Safety

Nuclear safety focuses on preventing accidents, controlling hazards, protecting people and the environment, and managing radioactive materials throughout the nuclear lifecycle.

## Scope

- Radiation safety
- Reactor safety
- Decay heat
- Containment
- Nuclear accidents
- Nuclear waste
