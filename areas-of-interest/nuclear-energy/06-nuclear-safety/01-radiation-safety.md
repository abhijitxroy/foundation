# Radiation Safety

Radiation safety is the practice of protecting people and the environment from harmful exposure to ionizing radiation.

## Ionizing Radiation

Ionizing radiation has enough energy to remove electrons from atoms.

Examples:

- Alpha particles
- Beta particles
- Gamma rays
- X-rays
- Neutrons

## Main Protection Principles

The basic principles are:

```text
Time       → Minimize exposure time
Distance   → Maximize distance
Shielding  → Use appropriate shielding
