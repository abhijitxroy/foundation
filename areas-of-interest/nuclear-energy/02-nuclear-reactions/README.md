# Nuclear Reactions

Nuclear reactions involve changes to atomic nuclei and can release or absorb significant amounts of energy.

## Scope

- Nuclear fission
- Nuclear fusion
- Chain reactions
- Criticality
- Neutron capture
- Fissile materials
- Fertile materials
- Nuclear breeding
