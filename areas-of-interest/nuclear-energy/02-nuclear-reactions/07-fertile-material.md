# Fertile Material

Fertile material is nuclear material that is not itself readily fissile but can be converted into a fissile isotope through neutron capture and subsequent nuclear transformations.

## Important Fertile Isotopes

- **U-238**
- **Th-232**

## U-238 → Pu-239

The basic pathway is:

U-238
  ↓ neutron capture
U-239
  ↓ beta decay
