# Tritium Breeding

Tritium breeding is the process of producing tritium inside a fusion reactor so that the reactor can replenish the tritium consumed by deuterium-tritium (D-T) fusion.

## Why Tritium Breeding Is Needed

Tritium is radioactive and has a short half-life, so large natural supplies do not exist.

A practical D-T fusion plant therefore needs an internal tritium fuel cycle.

```text
D-T Fusion
    ↓
Tritium Consumption
    ↓
Tritium Must Be Replaced
    ↓
Breeding
    ↓
New Tritium
