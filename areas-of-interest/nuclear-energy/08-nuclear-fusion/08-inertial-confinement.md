# Inertial Confinement

Inertial confinement fusion (ICF) is a fusion approach that compresses a small fuel capsule rapidly to create the temperature and pressure required for fusion.

## Basic Principle

```text
Fusion Fuel Capsule
        ↓
Intense Energy Pulse
        ↓
Rapid Compression
        ↓
Extreme Temperature + Pressure
        ↓
Fusion
        ↓
Energy
