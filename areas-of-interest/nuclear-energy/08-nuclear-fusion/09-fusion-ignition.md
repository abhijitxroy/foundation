# Fusion Ignition

Fusion ignition is a condition in which the energy deposited into the fusion fuel by fusion-produced particles is sufficient to sustain the fusion burn.

## Basic Principle

```text
Fusion Fuel
    ↓
Compression / Heating
    ↓
Fusion Reactions
    ↓
Charged Fusion Products
    ↓
Self-Heating
    ↓
More Fusion
