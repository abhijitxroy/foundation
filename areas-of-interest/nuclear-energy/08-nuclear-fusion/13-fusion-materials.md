# Fusion Materials

Fusion materials are the structural and functional materials used to build a fusion reactor and withstand extreme temperature, radiation, neutron, and mechanical conditions.

## Why Materials Are Critical

A fusion reactor must operate in an environment involving:

- Very high temperatures
- High-energy neutrons
- Strong heat fluxes
- Plasma exposure
- Radiation damage
- Thermal cycling
