# Thorium Challenges

Thorium has significant potential as a nuclear fuel, but a practical thorium fuel cycle has major technical, economic, and infrastructure challenges.

## 1. Th-232 Is Not Fissile

Thorium-232 cannot normally sustain the required fission chain reaction by itself.
