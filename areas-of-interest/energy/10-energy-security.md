# Energy Security

Energy security is the ability to provide reliable, affordable, and sufficient energy despite disruptions or changing conditions.

## Why It Matters

Modern economies depend on continuous energy supply.

Energy disruptions can affect:

- Electricity
- Transportation
- Industry
- Food production
- Communication
- Defence
- Economic activity

## Main Dimensions

### Availability

Is sufficient energy available?

### Affordability

Can consumers and industries obtain it at an acceptable cost?

### Reliability

Can energy be supplied when required?

### Resilience

Can the system withstand and recover from disruptions?

### Sustainability

Can the system continue operating over the long term without unacceptable environmental or resource impacts?

## Major Risks

Energy security can be affected by:

- Import dependence
- Geopolitical conflicts
- Supply-chain disruptions
- Resource shortages
- Price volatility
- Infrastructure failures
- Natural disasters
- Cyberattacks
- Extreme weather
- Political instability
