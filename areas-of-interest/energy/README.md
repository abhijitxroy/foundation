# Energy

Energy is the foundation of modern civilization, enabling electricity, transportation, industry, infrastructure, communication, and economic activity.

## Scope

This section covers the fundamentals of energy systems:

- Energy and power
- Electricity generation
- Energy sources
- Energy density
- Capacity factor
- Baseload and dispatchable power
- Grid stability
- Energy storage
- Energy security
- Energy independence
- Energy transition
