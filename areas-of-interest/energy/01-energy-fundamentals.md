# Energy Fundamentals

Energy is the capacity to do work or cause change.

Modern civilization depends on energy for:

- Electricity
- Transportation
- Industry
- Heating and cooling
- Agriculture
- Communication
- Computing
- Defence and infrastructure

## Energy vs Power

**Energy** = how much energy is produced, stored, or consumed.

**Power** = how quickly energy is produced, transferred, or consumed.

`Power = Energy / Time`

| Quantity | Units |
|---|---|
| Energy | J, kWh, MWh, GWh |
| Power | W, kW, MW, GW |

### Example

A 1 MW generator operating for 1 hour produces:

`1 MW × 1 hour = 1 MWh`

So:

`MW → rate`

`MWh → amount`

## Forms of Energy

- **Kinetic** — energy of motion
- **Potential** — stored energy due to position or configuration
- **Thermal** — energy associated with temperature and microscopic motion
- **Chemical** — energy stored in chemical bonds
- **Electrical** — energy associated with electric charge and fields
- **Radiant** — energy carried by electromagnetic radiation
- **Nuclear** — energy associated with atomic nuclei

Energy can be converted from one form to another.
