# Energy Transition

Energy transition is the long-term shift from one energy system to another.

It involves changes in:

- Energy sources
- Technologies
- Infrastructure
- Transportation
- Industry
- Electricity grids
- Storage
- Energy consumption

## Why Energy Systems Transition

Major drivers include:

- Resource availability
- Energy security
- Cost
- Technology improvements
- Environmental impact
- Climate goals
- Industrial development
- Geopolitical conditions
