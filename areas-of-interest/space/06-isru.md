# In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU)

In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU) means using resources available at a mission destination instead of transporting all required materials from Earth.

## Basic Principle

```text
Earth
  ↓
Transport Equipment
  ↓
Moon / Mars / Other Body
  ↓
Local Resources
  ↓
Processing
  ↓
Useful Products
