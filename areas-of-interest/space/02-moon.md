# Moon

The Moon is Earth's natural satellite and the closest major celestial body to Earth.

## Basic Facts

- Distance from Earth: ~384,400 km
- Diameter: ~3,475 km
- Gravity: ~16.5% of Earth's gravity
- Orbital period: ~27.3 days
- Rotation period: ~27.3 days
- Atmosphere: Extremely thin exosphere
