# Lunar Mining

Lunar mining is the extraction and processing of useful resources from the Moon.

## Main Targets

Potential lunar mining targets include:

- Water ice
- Oxygen-bearing minerals
- Iron
- Aluminum
- Silicon
- Titanium
- Other mineral resources
- Helium-3
