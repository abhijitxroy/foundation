# Common Glossary

A collection of important non-engineering terms and concepts used throughout the repository. Definitions are intentionally concise and designed for quick reference and revision.

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Astronomy** | The scientific study of celestial objects, space, and the universe. |
| **Budget** | A financial plan that estimates income and expenses over a period of time. |
| **Capitalism** | An economic system where the means of production are primarily privately owned and operated for profit. |
| **Democracy** | A system of government in which power is exercised by the people, directly or through elected representatives. |
| **Economics** | The study of how individuals, businesses, and societies allocate scarce resources. |
| **Geography** | The study of the Earth's landscapes, environments, and the relationships between people and places. |
| **Inflation** | A sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services over time. |
| **Investment** | The act of allocating money or resources with the expectation of generating future returns. |
| **Market Capitalization** | The total market value of a company's outstanding shares. |
| **Parliament** | The legislative body responsible for making laws and overseeing the government in a parliamentary system. |
| **Politics** | The activities and processes associated with governance, public policy, and the distribution of power. |
| **Science** | The systematic pursuit of knowledge through observation, experimentation, and reasoning. |
| **Sovereignty** | The supreme authority of a state to govern itself without external interference. |
| **Stock Market** | A marketplace where shares of publicly traded companies are bought and sold. |
| **Universe** | The entirety of space, time, matter, energy, and all celestial objects. |

## Purpose

- Maintain a quick-reference dictionary for general knowledge concepts.
- Keep definitions short, practical, and easy to revise.
- Provide a shared terminology base across non-engineering topics in the repository.
