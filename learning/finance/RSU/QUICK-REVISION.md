## Before Joining

Always understand:

- Base salary.
- Joining bonus.
- RSUs.
- Vesting schedule.
- Annual refreshers.
- Promotion grants.
- Compensation review cycles.
- Benefits and relocation policies.

Never negotiate:
> Base salary alone.

---

## After Joining

Understand:

- Performance expectations.
- Compensation cycles.
- Career growth opportunities.
- Promotion expectations.
- Future stock grant opportunities.

Never assume:
> Good performance automatically guarantees additional RSUs.

---

## Questions to Ask Your Manager

Consider asking:

- Am I meeting expectations at my current level?
- What additional impact is expected from me?
- What differentiates top performers?
- Which skills should I improve?
- What should I focus on over the next 6-12 months?
- What additional responsibilities should I take ownership of?

Avoid:

```text
Compensation Decisions

↓

Already Finalized

↓

Employee Requests Promotion

↓

Too Late.
```


Prefer:

```text
Quarterly Discussions

↓

Career Discussions

↓

Growth Discussions

↓

Compensation Discussions

↓

Promotion Discussions

↓

Compensation Cycle

↓

Refreshers

↓

Future Stock Grants
```

Always discuss growth opportunities before compensation decisions are finalized.

---

## Vesting Decisions

After shares vest:

```text
Shares Received
       |
-------------------------
|           |            |
Hold       Sell      Partial Sell
|           |            |
Long Term  Diversify   Balanced
Investing              Approach
```

All three approaches are valid.

The appropriate strategy depends upon:
- Financial goals.
- Risk tolerance.
- Portfolio allocation.
- Employer concentration risk.

---

## Taxation Checklist

Understand:

- When taxation applies.
- Capital gains implications.
- Employer withholding policies.
- Annual tax filing requirements.
- Country-specific tax regulations.

Always review:
- Payslips.
- Tax statements.
- Brokerage statements.

## Before Leaving the Company

Always verify:

- Upcoming vesting dates.
- Unvested RSUs.
- Tax implications.
- Separation agreements.
- Brokerage account information.
- Company-specific policies.

Never resign without understanding:
> The compensation implications of your decision.

---

## Common Mistakes

Avoid:

- Negotiating only base salary.
- Ignoring annual refreshers.
- Ignoring compensation cycles.
- Waiting for growth discussions to happen automatically.
- Ignoring taxation implications.
- Holding every vested share indefinitely.
- Ignoring concentration risk.
- Ignoring upcoming vesting dates before leaving the company.

---

## Quick Checklist

### Before Joining

- Understand the complete compensation package.
- Understand vesting schedules.
- Understand refreshers and promotion grants.

### After Joining

- Understand performance expectations.
- Track achievements and business impact.
- Understand compensation cycles.

### Before Compensation Discussions

- Understand growth expectations.
- Discuss future opportunities proactively.

### Before Vesting

- Understand taxation implications.
- Decide whether to hold, sell, or partially sell.

### Before Leaving

- Review vested and unvested shares.
- Review upcoming vesting events.
- Understand company-specific exit policies.

> ### Remember
>
> RSUs are not merely stock grants. Treat them as a combination of compensation, career growth, tax planning, and wealth creation. Understanding when to discuss growth opportunities, how to manage vested shares, and how RSUs fit into your overall financial strategy can materially impact long-term compensation.
