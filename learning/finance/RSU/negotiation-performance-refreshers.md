# Negotiation, Performance and Refreshers

> Good performance does not automatically guarantee promotions or additional stock grants. Understanding compensation cycles, manager expectations, and career discussions is equally important.

---

## Before Joining

Never negotiate only:

```text
Base Salary
```

Always understand the complete compensation package:

```text
Base Salary
      +
Joining Bonus
      +
RSUs
      +
Annual Refreshers
      +
Promotion Grants
      +
Benefits
      +
Relocation Benefits
      +
Career Growth Opportunities
```

---

## Questions to Ask HR

Understand:

- Is RSU included in the compensation package?
- What is the vesting schedule?
- Are annual refreshers provided?
- Are promotion grants available?
- What is the compensation review cycle?
- What benefits are included?
- Are there any retention or performance grants?

Never assume:
> Good performance automatically guarantees additional RSUs.

## Compensation Discussions

Never wait for:

```text
Manager

↓

You deserve additional RSUs.
```


Prefer:

```text
Understand:

- Performance expectations.
- Promotion criteria.
- Compensation cycles.
- Refreshers.
- Career growth opportunities.
```

Employees should proactively understand:
- What differentiates good performers from top performers?
- What additional impact is expected at their level?
- Which skills should they improve?
- What is expected for future growth opportunities?

## Questions to Ask Your Manager

Consider asking:

- Am I meeting expectations at my current level?
- What differentiates top performers at my level?
- What additional impact is expected from me?
- Which skills should I improve over the next 6-12 months?
- What additional responsibilities should I take ownership of?
- What should I focus on to prepare for future growth opportunities?

---

## Annual Refreshers

Additional RSUs may be provided based on:
- Company policies.
- Performance.
- Promotions.
- Business priorities.
- Compensation philosophies.

Understand:
- When refresher discussions typically happen.
- What expectations exist at your level.
- What business impact is valued.
- What additional responsibilities may be required.

---

## Common Mistakes

Avoid:

- Negotiating only base salary.
- Waiting for compensation discussions to happen automatically.
- Ignoring compensation cycles.
- Ignoring annual refreshers.
- Ignoring promotion expectations.
- Failing to document achievements and business impact.
- Assuming good performance automatically guarantees additional stock grants.

## Understanding Eligibility

Employees should understand:

- Am I eligible for RSUs at my current level?
- Are annual refreshers available at my level?
- Are promotion grants available?
- How are compensation decisions made?
- What additional expectations exist at higher levels?

Understanding eligibility requirements early can help employees better plan their career growth and compensation discussions.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Understand the complete compensation package before joining.
- Compensation discussions should happen well before compensation decisions are finalized.
- Career growth requires both strong performance and effective communication.
- Annual refreshers and promotion grants can significantly impact long-term compensation.
- Regular career discussions are often more valuable than waiting for annual reviews.

> ### Rule of Thumb
>
> Don't wait for compensation discussions to find you. Understand expectations early, discuss growth opportunities proactively, and align your contributions with long-term career goals.
