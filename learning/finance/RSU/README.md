# Restricted Stock Units (RSU)

> Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) are company-provided stock grants that form an important part of employee compensation and long-term wealth creation.

RSUs should not be viewed merely as stock grants. Employees should understand how they affect:
- Compensation.
- Career growth.
- Performance discussions.
- Promotions.
- Annual refreshers.
- Taxation.
- Investment decisions.
- Exit planning.

---

## Learning Objectives

This module helps answer the following questions:

- What are RSUs and how do they work?
- How should I negotiate RSUs while joining a company?
- When should I discuss promotions, refreshers, and compensation with my manager?
- How do vesting schedules work?
- What are the taxation implications of RSUs?
- Should I hold or sell vested shares?
- What happens if I leave the company?
- How should RSUs fit into my overall investment strategy?

## Most Common Mistakes

Avoid:

- Negotiating only base salary.
- Waiting for compensation discussions to happen automatically.
- Ignoring annual refreshers.
- Ignoring compensation cycles.
- Ignoring taxation implications.
- Holding every vested share indefinitely.
- Ignoring concentration risk.
- Failing to document achievements and business impact.
