# Leaving the Company

> Leaving a company can significantly impact your RSUs. Employees should understand what happens to both vested and unvested shares before making career decisions.

---

## Common Exit Scenarios

Leaving a company may occur because of:

- Resignation.
- Layoffs.
- Retirement.
- Termination.
- Mutual separation agreements.
- Company-specific exit programs.

Always verify:
- Your employment agreement.
- Company RSU policies.
- Vesting schedules.
- Country-specific taxation requirements.

---

```text
        |                      |
     Employee             Future Vesting
      Owns                   Required
        |
     Leaving Company
```

Generally speaking:

```text
Vested Shares
      |

the employee.


------------------------


Unvested Shares
       |

continued employment.
```

Always verify:
- Company policies.
- Employment agreements.
- Separation terms.
- Country-specific requirements.

## Resignation

```text
       Resignation
             |
      Leaving Company
             |
     --------------------
     |                  |
Vested Shares      Unvested Shares
     |                  |
Generally Keep      Generally Forfeit
```

Employees should understand:
- Last working day.
- Vesting dates.
- Tax implications.
- Brokerage account policies.

A few days may significantly impact:
- Upcoming vesting events.
- Compensation decisions.
- Future stock grants.

Always understand the timing implications before resigning.

---

## Layoffs

Company policies may differ.

Possible outcomes include:

```text
         Layoff
            |
     -------------------
     |                 |
Accelerated          Normal
  Vesting            Vesting
     |                 |
Company Policy      Company Policy
     |                 |
Additional          Unvested Shares
Protection          May Be Forfeited
```

Some companies may provide:
- Additional vesting periods.
- Accelerated vesting.
- Separation benefits.
- Special retention arrangements.

Never assume all companies follow the same policies.

## Promotion and Transfers

Employees should understand:

- Promotion grants.

- Country-specific taxation implications.
- Compensation cycle impacts.

Promotions and transfers may affect:
- Future stock grants.
- Compensation structures.
- Career growth opportunities.

---

## Common Mistakes

Avoid:

- Ignoring upcoming vesting dates.
- Ignoring taxation implications.
- Assuming all unvested shares will vest.
- Making resignation decisions without understanding compensation implications.
- Forgetting to review brokerage account information.
- Ignoring company-specific policies.

---

## Leaving Company Checklist

Before leaving:

- Review all vested shares.
- Review all unvested shares.
- Verify upcoming vesting dates.
- Understand taxation implications.
- Review brokerage account information.
- Understand company-specific exit policies.
- Understand compensation implications.
- Maintain copies of relevant documentation.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Understand the difference between vested and unvested shares.
- Company policies may significantly impact RSUs after separation.
- Always review upcoming vesting dates before making career decisions.
- Never assume all companies follow identical policies.
- Taxation and brokerage requirements should be reviewed before leaving.

> ### Rule of Thumb
>
> Before making any decision to leave a company, understand how it affects your vested shares, unvested shares, taxation, and upcoming vesting events. A well-timed career decision can materially impact long-term compensation and wealth creation.
