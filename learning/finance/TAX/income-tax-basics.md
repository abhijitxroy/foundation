# Income Tax Basics

## Income Tax

Income tax is a tax charged on taxable income according to the applicable tax rules.

## Taxable Income

Taxable income is the amount of income considered for taxation after applying the deductions, exemptions and adjustments allowed under the applicable tax regime.
