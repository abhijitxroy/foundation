# Surcharge and Cess

## Surcharge

Surcharge is an additional tax charged on the **income tax already calculated** for higher-income taxpayers.

It is **not calculated directly on income**.

### Example

If income tax = ₹10,00,000 and surcharge = 10%:

```text
Surcharge = ₹10,00,000 × 10%
          = ₹1,00,000
