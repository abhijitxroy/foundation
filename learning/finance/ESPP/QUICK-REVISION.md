---

## Before Enrollment

Understand:

- Eligibility requirements.
- Contribution limits.
- Discount percentage.
- Purchase dates.
- Look-back feature.
- Tax implications.
- Hold or sell strategy.
- Company-specific ESPP policies.

---

## Contribution Percentage Selection

| Contribution | Best For |
|--------------|----------|
| 2% | Beginners |
| 5% | Conservative approach |
| 10% | Balanced approach |
| 15% | Excellent cash flow and maximum participation |

Always ask:

```text
Can I comfortably afford
the deduction throughout the
entire offering period?

↓

YES

↓

Proceed.


NO

↓

Reduce contribution percentage.
```

---

## Before Choosing Maximum Contribution

Ask yourself:

- Emergency fund available?
- Comfortable cash flow?
- Major expenses approaching?
- Significant debt obligations?
- Portfolio adequately diversified?
- Understand company policies?
- Comfortable with stock market risks?

If most answers are YES:
> Maximum contribution may be reasonable.

## Look-back Feature

Without Look-back:

```text
$100

↓

15% Discount

↓

$85
```


With Look-back:

```text
Offering Date
↓

$70

↓

Purchase Date
↓

$100

↓

Lower Price Selected

↓

$70

↓

15% Discount

↓

$59.50
```

Look-back always favors employees.

## Hold or Sell?

Possible approaches include:

```text
Purchase Shares
       |
-------------------------
|           |            |
Hold       Sell      Partial Sell
|           |            |
Long Term  Diversify   Balanced
Investing              Approach
```

The appropriate strategy depends upon:
- Financial goals.
- Risk tolerance.
- Portfolio allocation.
- Employer concentration risk.

---

Avoid:

```text

   +

   +

   +
No Diversification

↓

Everything depends on one company.
```


Prefer:

```text
Salary
   +
ESPP
   +
Diversified Portfolio

↓

Index Funds
+
Flexicap Funds
+
Debt Allocation
+

```

## If I Leave the Company

Depending upon company policies:

```text
Leave Company
       |
Before Purchase Date
       |
Contributions Refunded
       |
No Shares Purchased
```


Always verify:
- Purchase date eligibility.
- Withdrawal policies.
- Contribution refund policies.
- Country-specific taxation implications.

---

## Common Mistakes

Avoid:

- Selecting the maximum contribution without proper planning.
- Ignoring monthly cash flow requirements.
- Ignoring concentration risk.
- Ignoring taxation implications.
- Investing based upon recent stock price movements.
- Following colleagues' investment decisions.
- Ignoring company-specific ESPP policies.

---

## Enrollment Checklist

- Understand company policies.
- Verify eligibility requirements.
- Understand contribution limits.
- Understand purchase dates.
- Verify whether Look-back exists.
- Decide the contribution percentage.
- Decide the hold or sell strategy.
- Review taxation implications.
- Maintain adequate emergency savings.

---

```text
Good Cash Flow
```

> ### Remember
>
> Never select a contribution percentage that compromises your financial stability. ESPP should complement your long-term wealth creation strategy rather than become your entire investment strategy.
