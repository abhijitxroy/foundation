# Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)

> ESPP allows eligible employees to purchase their employer's stock at a discounted price through payroll deductions.

## Learning Objectives

This module helps answer the following questions:

- What is ESPP?
- Who is eligible to participate?
- How does ESPP work?
- How much should I contribute (2%-15%)?
- What is the Look-back feature?
- When should I choose maximum contribution?
- What are the risks involved?
- Should I hold or sell the purchased shares?
- What happens if I leave the company before the purchase date?
- How should ESPP fit into my overall investment strategy?

## Important Decisions

Before enrolling in an ESPP, understand:

- Contribution percentage.
- Discount percentage.
- Look-back feature.
- Vesting and purchase dates.
- Cash-flow implications.
- Tax implications.
- Hold vs sell strategy.
- Concentration risk.
- Exit scenarios (resignation, layoff, etc.).

## Rule of Thumb

> Maximize the employee benefit only when it does not compromise your cash flow, emergency fund, or portfolio diversification.
