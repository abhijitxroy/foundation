# Look-back Feature

> The Look-back feature is one of the most valuable benefits offered by some Employee Stock Purchase Plans (ESPPs). It allows employees to purchase shares based on the lower stock price between the offering date and the purchase date, along with the applicable discount.

---

## What is the Look-back Feature?

Without a Look-back feature:

```text
Purchase Date Price
        |
      $100
        |
   15% Discount
        |
      $85
        |
 Purchase Price
```


With a Look-back feature:

```text
Offering Date Price
        |
       $70
        |
Purchase Date Price
        |
      $100
        |
 Lower Price Selected
        |
       $70
        |
   15% Discount
        |
      $59.50
        |
 Purchase Price
```

The employee potentially benefits from:
- Lower purchase price.
- Purchase discount.
- Stock price appreciation during the offering period.

---

## How Does It Work?

A company supporting the Look-back feature typically compares:

- Offering date stock price.
- Purchase date stock price.

The lower price is selected before applying the discount.

```text
Offering Date
       |
      $80
       |
Purchase Date
       |
     $120
       |
Lower Price Selected
       |
      $80
       |
15% Discount Applied
       |
      $68
       |
Purchase Price
```

---

## Why is the Look-back Feature Valuable?

Employees potentially benefit from:

- Lower purchase price.
- Purchase discount.
- Stock price appreciation.
- More favorable purchase economics compared to plans without the Look-back feature.

The Look-back feature can significantly improve the economics of an ESPP.

## Is the Look-back Feature Always Beneficial?

Yes.

From an employee's perspective, the Look-back feature is highly beneficial.

Whether the stock price:
- Increases,
- Decreases, or
- Remains unchanged,

the Look-back feature always favors the employee by selecting the lower eligible stock price before applying the discount.

However, employees should still consider:
- Concentration risk.
- Cash-flow implications.
- Portfolio diversification.
- Tax implications.
- Overall investment strategy.

## How Can I Verify if My ESPP Provides a Look-back Feature?

Review the company's:

- ESPP plan documents.
- Prospectus.
- Enrollment documents.
- Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs).

Look for phrases such as:

```text
- Lower of the Offering Date or Purchase Date price.

- Lower of the Fair Market Value.

- Lesser of the Offering Date price or Purchase Date price.

- 85% of the lower market value.
```

If the documentation states:

```text
85% of the Fair Market Value
on the Purchase Date.
```

then the plan most likely does NOT provide a Look-back feature.

## Evaluating an ESPP

| Parameter | Good | Better | Excellent |
|-----------|------|--------|-----------|
| Discount | 5% | 10% | 15%+ |
| Look-back Feature | No | Optional | Yes |
| Immediate Sell Allowed | No | Optional | Yes |
| Contribution Refund | No | Optional | Yes |
| Contribution Limit | 5% | 10% | 15%+ |

An ESPP providing:
- 15% discount,
- Look-back feature,
- Immediate sell options,
- Contribution refunds, and
- Higher contribution limits,

is generally considered an excellent employee benefit.

---

## Key Takeaways

- The Look-back feature allows employees to benefit from the lower stock price between the offering date and purchase date.
- It is one of the most valuable ESPP benefits available.
- It always favors employees irrespective of stock price movement.
- Always verify whether your company's ESPP includes this feature.
- Understand the discount percentage, contribution limits, and taxation implications before enrolling.

> ### Rule of Thumb
>
> When evaluating an ESPP, pay close attention to the Look-back feature. A plan offering both a significant discount and the Look-back feature is substantially more attractive than one offering a discount alone.
