# Look-back Feature (ESPP)

## What is it?
The Look-back feature allows ESPP shares to be purchased at:
> **85% (15% discount) of the LOWER price between:**
> - Offering/Enrollment Date price, OR
> - Purchase Date price.

## Why is it useful?
- Protects employees when the stock price rises.
- Provides additional gains beyond the 15% discount.
- Works in your favor whether the stock price goes up or down.

## Example

| Date | Stock Price |
|------|------------|
| Offering Date | $30 |
| Purchase Date | $50 |

Without Look-back:
> Purchase Price = $42.50 ($50 - 15%)

With Look-back:
> Lower Price = $30  
> Purchase Price = $25.50 ($30 - 15%)

Market Price = $50

> Gain = $24.50/share (~96%)

## Formula

> Purchase Price = 85% × MIN(Offering Date Price, Purchase Date Price)

## Is it Good?
> YES. The Look-back feature is one of the most valuable benefits of an ESPP.

## How to Verify?
Search the ESPP Plan Documents for:
- "lower of"
- "85%"
- "Offering Date"
- "Purchase Date"

If it says:

> "85% of the lower of the Fair Market Value on the Offering Date or Purchase Date"

→ **Look-back Feature = YES**

If it says:

> "85% of the Fair Market Value on the Purchase Date"

→ **Look-back Feature = NO**

> **Rule of Thumb:**  
> 15% Discount = Very Good  
> 15% Discount + Look-back = Outstanding ESPP Benefit
