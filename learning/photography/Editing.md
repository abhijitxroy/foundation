# Editing

## Overview

This document contains practical notes for editing photos. The goal is to improve a photo while keeping it natural.

## Editing Principles

- Edit only when needed
- Keep natural colors
- Avoid over-editing
- Keep the original photo

## Editing Checklist

- Crop if needed
- Straighten the horizon
- Adjust exposure
- Adjust highlights and shadows
- Adjust colors
- Reduce noise if needed
- Sharpen moderately
- Export in the required quality
