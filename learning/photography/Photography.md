# Photography

## Overview

Photography is about using light to capture a moment and tell a story. No matter which camera you use, the same basic photography principles apply.

## Core Principles

- Light
- Composition
- Perspective
- Timing
- Storytelling

## Composition

### Rule of Thirds
- Place the subject near one of the four intersection points.

### Center Composition
- Works well for symmetry and subjects in the center.

### Leading Lines
- Use lines to guide the viewer's eyes to the subject.

### Foreground Interest
- Add foreground objects to create depth.

### Human Element
- Add a person to show scale and make the photo more interesting.

## Light

- Natural Light
- Golden Hour
- Blue Hour
- Indoor Light
- Backlighting
- Reflections

## Perspective

Before taking a photo, ask:

- Can I move closer?
- Can I move farther?
- Can I shoot lower?
- Can I shoot higher?
- Can I move left or right?

## Focus

- Keep the main subject in focus.
- Tap to focus when needed.
- Lock focus for still subjects.

## Exposure

Exposure controls how bright or dark a photo looks.

## White Balance

White balance helps keep colors natural under different lighting conditions.

## HDR & Dynamic Range

Use HDR when a scene has both very bright and very dark areas.

## Image Formats

- HEIF
- JPEG
- RAW

## Photography Checklist

- Clean the lens
- Check the light
- Compose the frame
- Focus on the subject
- Capture multiple angles
- Review important shots before leaving

## Common Mistakes

- Crooked horizon
- Distracting background
- Poor lighting
- Camera shake
- Over-editing
- Excessive digital zoom
