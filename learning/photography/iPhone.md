## Practical Tips
- Hold the iPhone upside down for low-angle shots and reflections.
- Pre-focus before capturing moving subjects.
- Clean the camera lenses before every shoot.
- Move closer instead of using digital zoom whenever possible.
- Take photos from different angles before leaving.
