# Reading Methodology

## Purpose

This document defines the standard approach for reading books and other learning resources within the Personal Knowledge Ecosystem (PKE). The objective is to transform information into long-term, reusable knowledge rather than simply completing books.

---

## Reading Principles

- Read with a clear objective.
- Prioritize understanding over speed.
- Focus on quality rather than quantity.
- Take notes in your own words.
- Revisit important books periodically.
- Connect new ideas with existing knowledge.

---

## Reading Workflow

1. Define why the resource is being read.
2. Understand the author's background and context.
3. Read actively while highlighting important ideas.
4. Capture key concepts using the Book Note Template.
5. Record actionable insights and questions.
6. Cross-reference related books, people, events, and concepts.
7. Summarize the book in your own words.
8. Review notes periodically to reinforce learning.

---

## Reading Categories

Different types of material may require different approaches:

- Technical books
- History and research books
- Biographies
- Business and finance
- Philosophy and psychology
- Reference books

---

## Expected Outputs

Reading should produce one or more of the following:

- Structured book notes
- Key takeaways
- Memorable quotes
- Mental models and frameworks
- Cross references
- Reading recommendations
- Action items

---

## Status

This methodology will continue to evolve as additional books are read and better reading practices are developed through experience.
