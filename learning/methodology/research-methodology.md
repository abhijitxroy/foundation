# Research Methodology

## Purpose

This document defines the standard approach for conducting research throughout the Personal Knowledge Ecosystem (PKE). It aims to produce accurate, well-structured, verifiable, and reusable knowledge.

---

## Research Principles

- Start with primary sources whenever possible.
- Distinguish facts from opinions and interpretations.
- Cross-check important claims using multiple reliable sources.
- Record sources as research progresses rather than at the end.
- Preserve historical context and avoid present-day assumptions.
- Update conclusions when stronger evidence becomes available.

---

## Standard Research Workflow

1. Define the research objective.
2. Identify primary and secondary sources.
3. Collect and organize source material.
4. Verify facts across independent references.
5. Extract key findings and supporting evidence.
6. Organize findings into structured notes.
7. Link related topics across the ecosystem.
8. Record complete references.
9. Review for completeness and accuracy.
10. Publish the final documentation.

---

## Source Priority

1. Primary sources (official records, archival documents, original publications)
2. Academic books and peer-reviewed research
3. Reputable reference books
4. Government publications
5. Newspapers and magazines (used with context)
6. Reputable websites
7. Community sources (used only for discovery and verification leads)

---

## Expected Outputs

Depending on the project, research may produce:

- Timelines
- Biographies
- City or state documentation
- Documentary research
- Book bibliographies
- Maps and location references
- Cross-reference indexes

---

## Status

This methodology will continue to evolve as additional research projects are completed, ensuring that improvements are based on practical experience rather than theory alone.
