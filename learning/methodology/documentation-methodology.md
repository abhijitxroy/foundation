# Documentation Methodology

## Purpose

This document defines the standard approach for transforming research, learning, and experience into structured, maintainable, and reusable documentation throughout the Personal Knowledge Ecosystem (PKE).

The goal is not simply to write notes, but to create knowledge that remains understandable, discoverable, and valuable for years.

---

## Documentation Principles

- Document for your future self first.
- Prefer clarity over cleverness.
- Preserve existing knowledge before restructuring it.
- Organize information logically rather than chronologically.
- Keep documentation concise while linking to detailed references when needed.
- Continuously improve documents as knowledge evolves.

---

## Documentation Workflow

1. Define the purpose of the document.
2. Identify the intended audience (self, research, documentary, public).
3. Gather verified information and supporting references.
4. Organize content into a logical structure.
5. Add cross-references to related documents where appropriate.
6. Review for accuracy, readability, and consistency.
7. Update the document as new knowledge becomes available.

---

## Recommended Document Structure

Where applicable, documents should include:

- Title
- Purpose or overview
- Main content
- Related topics
- References
- Revision history (when appropriate)

---

## Writing Guidelines

- Use descriptive headings.
- Prefer short paragraphs and meaningful lists.
- Separate facts from opinions.
- Clearly identify assumptions or unresolved questions.
- Avoid duplication by linking to existing documentation.

---

## Status

This methodology will evolve alongside the Personal Knowledge Ecosystem as additional repositories, research projects, and documentary work are completed.
