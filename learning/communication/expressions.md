# Expressions

> A highly curated collection of timeless, practical, and naturally used expressions that improve everyday and professional communication.

## Inclusion Criteria

Only store expressions that are:

- Naturally spoken or written.
- Frequently useful in personal or professional life.
- Timeless and practical.
- Worth revisiting after 10-20 years.
- Helpful for communicating ideas more clearly and naturally.

Do NOT store:

- Rarely used idioms.
- Internet slang and temporary trends.
- Low-value or unnatural expressions.
- Copy-pasted collections.
- Temporary learning notes.
