# Concepts

> A highly curated collection of timeless concepts, mental models, psychological patterns, and thinking frameworks that improve decision making, communication, and personal growth.

## Inclusion Criteria

Only store concepts that are:

- Timeless and practical.
- Frequently applicable in personal or professional life.
- Helpful for better thinking and decision making.
- Worth revisiting after 10-20 years.
- Naturally acquired through meaningful learning.

Do NOT store:

- Temporary buzzwords.
- Internet trends.
- Low-value concepts.
- Duplicate or copy-pasted collections.
- Concepts that are unlikely to be used or revisited.

## Entry Template

| Field | Description |

| Where It Applies | Personal life, professional life, investing, decision making, etc. |
| Why Preserve It? | Lifetime value or personal significance. |
