# Vocabulary

A curated collection of high-frequency English vocabulary.

## Categories

- 📘 **Everyday** – Daily conversations, common vocabulary, and frequently used words.
- 💼 **Workplace** – Meetings, emails, leadership, collaboration, and professional communication.
- 📰 **Politics** – Government, elections, public policy, diplomacy, and international affairs.
- ⚖️ **Law & Crime** – Crime, policing, courts, investigations, and legal terminology.
- 🌍 **Society** – Culture, education, social issues, equality, diversity, and human behavior.
- 💰 **Finance** – Personal finance, investing, banking, business, and economics.
- 💻 **Technology** – Software engineering, AI, cloud computing, DevOps, cybersecurity, and system design.
- ✍️ **Grammar** – Idioms, phrasal verbs, collocations, connectors, and commonly confused words.
- 🎯 **Interview** – High-impact vocabulary frequently used in interviews and professional discussions.

## Inclusion Criteria

Only add words that are:

- Frequently used in news, blogs, books, podcasts, and social media.
- Useful in professional communication and interviews.
- Likely to be encountered in real life.

Avoid:
- Rare GRE/SAT words.
- Obsolete words.
- Highly specialized jargon unless commonly used.

## Word Format

Each word should include:

- Meaning
- Part of speech
- Simple example
- Common usage
- Synonyms (optional)
- Antonyms (optional)
- Notes (optional)
