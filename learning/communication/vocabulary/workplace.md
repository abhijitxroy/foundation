# Workplace

High-frequency workplace vocabulary commonly used in meetings, emails, project management, engineering, leadership, and professional communication.

| Word | POS | Common Forms | Meaning | Example | Common Contexts |
|------|-----|--------------|---------|---------|-----------------|
| Collaborate | Verb | collaborates, collaborated, collaborating | To work together toward a common goal. | Our teams collaborated on the product launch. | Meetings, Project Management |
| Align | Verb | aligns, aligned, aligning | To bring people or plans into agreement. | Let's align on the project priorities. | Meetings, Leadership |
| Stakeholder | Noun | stakeholders | A person or group affected by or interested in a project or decision. | Keep all stakeholders informed. | Project Management |
| Accountability | Noun | — | Being responsible for your actions and results. | Accountability builds trust within the team. | Leadership |
| Ownership | Noun | — | Taking full responsibility for a task or outcome. | She showed strong ownership of the release. | Engineering, Leadership |
| Escalate | Verb | escalates, escalated, escalating | To raise an issue to a higher authority for resolution. | Escalate the issue if it blocks the release. | Project Management |
| Clarify | Verb | clarifies, clarified, clarifying | To make something easier to understand. | Could you clarify the requirement? | Meetings, Communication |
| Consensus | Noun | — | General agreement among a group of people. | The team reached a consensus after discussion. | Meetings |
| Prioritize | Verb | prioritizes, prioritized, prioritizing | To decide what is most important. | We need to prioritize customer issues first. | Planning |
| Deadline | Noun | deadlines | The latest time by which something must be completed. | The deadline is next Friday. | Project Management |
