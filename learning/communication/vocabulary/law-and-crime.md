# Law & Crime

High-frequency law and crime vocabulary commonly found in news reports, court cases, documentaries, and daily discussions.

| Word | POS | Common Forms | Meaning | Example | Used In |
|------|-----|--------------|---------|---------|---------|
| Victim | Noun | victims | A person harmed by a crime, accident, or other event. | The victim reported the incident to the police. | Crime, News |
| Extortion | Noun | extortions | The crime of obtaining money or favors through threats or force. | The businessman was charged with extortion. | Crime, Law |
| Suspect | Noun / Verb | suspects, suspected, suspecting | A person believed to have committed a crime, or to believe something is likely. | Police arrested the main suspect. | Crime, Investigation |
| Witness | Noun / Verb | witnesses, witnessed, witnessing | A person who sees an event, especially a crime or accident. | A witness testified in court. | Crime, Law |
| Verdict | Noun | verdicts | The formal decision made by a jury or judge in a court case. | The jury delivered a guilty verdict. | Law, Courts |
| Convict | Verb / Noun | convicts, convicted, convicting | To declare someone guilty of a crime; a person found guilty. | He was convicted of fraud. | Law, Courts |
| Fraud | Noun | frauds | Wrongful or criminal deception for financial or personal gain. | The company was investigated for fraud. | Crime, Business |
| Bribery | Noun | briberies | The act of offering or accepting money or favors to influence decisions illegally. | The official was arrested for bribery. | Crime, Politics |
