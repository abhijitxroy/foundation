### Idioms

| Expression | Meaning | Example | Used In |
|------------|---------|---------|---------|
| Skin in the game | Having a personal stake in the outcome. | Every investor should have skin in the game. | Business, Everyday |
| On the flip side | Looking at the opposite point of view. | On the flip side, the new policy has some benefits. | Conversation, Writing |
| Trial and error | Learning by trying different approaches. | We solved the issue through trial and error. | Everyday, Workplace |
| Draw a line in the sand | Set a firm limit that cannot be crossed. | Management drew a line in the sand on security standards. | Workplace |
| Make a dent | Make noticeable progress or have an impact. | We finally made a dent in the backlog. | Workplace |
