# Finance

High-frequency finance vocabulary commonly used in personal finance, investing, banking, business, the stock market, and economic news.

| Word | POS | Common Forms | Meaning | Example | Used In |
|------|-----|--------------|---------|---------|---------|
| Asset | Noun | assets | Something valuable that a person or business owns. | A house is considered an asset. | Investing, Accounting |
| Liability | Noun | liabilities | A debt or financial obligation. | The loan appears as a liability on the balance sheet. | Accounting |
| Budget | Noun / Verb | budgets, budgeted, budgeting | A financial plan for income and expenses. | We created a monthly budget to control spending. | Personal Finance |
| Investment | Noun | investments | Money invested to earn future returns. | Mutual funds are a popular investment. | Investing |
| Portfolio | Noun | portfolios | A collection of investments owned by a person or organization. | She diversified her investment portfolio. | Investing |
| Dividend | Noun | dividends | A portion of a company's profit paid to shareholders. | The company announced a quarterly dividend. | Stock Market |
| Inflation | Noun | — | A general rise in prices over time. | Inflation reduced consumers' purchasing power. | Economy |
| Recession | Noun | recessions | A period of declining economic activity. | Many businesses struggled during the recession. | Economy |
| Interest | Noun | interests | The cost of borrowing money or the return earned on savings. | The bank increased the interest rate. | Banking |
| Equity | Noun | equities | Ownership value in a company or an asset. | She invested in equity mutual funds. | Investing, Accounting |
