# Interview

High-impact vocabulary commonly used in behavioral interviews, system design discussions, technical presentations, and professional communication.

| Word | POS | Common Forms | Meaning | Example | Used In |
|------|-----|--------------|---------|---------|---------|
| Articulate | Verb / Adjective | articulates, articulated, articulating | To express ideas clearly and effectively. | She articulated her design decisions with confidence. | Interviews, Presentations |
| Concise | Adjective | — | Giving complete information using few words. | Keep your interview answers concise. | Interviews, Communication |
| Ownership | Noun | — | Taking full responsibility for a task or outcome. | I took ownership of the production issue until it was resolved. | Behavioral Interviews |
| Initiative | Noun | initiatives | The ability to act without being told. | He showed initiative by automating the deployment process. | Leadership |
| Impact | Noun / Verb | impacts, impacted, impacting | A strong effect or influence. | The optimization had a significant impact on performance. | Interviews, Workplace |
| Strategy | Noun | strategies | A long-term plan to achieve a goal. | Our migration strategy reduced deployment risks. | System Design |
| Trade-off | Noun | trade-offs | A balance between competing advantages and disadvantages. | Every architecture decision involves trade-offs. | System Design |
| Optimize | Verb | optimizes, optimized, optimizing | To improve efficiency or performance. | We optimized the build pipeline to reduce execution time. | Engineering |
| Scalable | Adjective | more scalable, most scalable | Able to handle increasing demand efficiently. | We designed a scalable microservices architecture. | System Design |
| Resilient | Adjective | more resilient, most resilient | Able to recover quickly from failures or problems. | The platform is resilient to node failures. | Distributed Systems |
