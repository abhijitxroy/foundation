# Words

> A highly curated collection of timeless, practical, and frequently useful words worth preserving for a lifetime.

## Inclusion Criteria

Only store words that are:

- Naturally usable.
- Frequently useful.
- Professionally valuable.
- Personally meaningful.
- Timeless.
- Worth revisiting after 10-20 years.

Do NOT store:

- Dictionary dumps.
- Rarely used vocabulary.
- GRE/IELTS/TOEFL word collections.
- Temporary notes.
- Internet collections.
- AI-generated fillers.
- Low-value additions.

---

## Entry Template

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Word | The word being preserved. |
| Meaning | A simple and practical meaning. |
| When to Use | Daily, professional, writing, meetings, interviews, etc. |
| Example | One natural example sentence. |
| Why Preserve It? (Optional) | Lifetime value, professional usefulness, or personal meaning. |
