# Knowledge Inclusion Framework

> "Curated over Collected. Practical over Impressive. Timeless over Temporary."

## Primary Objective

This module exists to preserve lifelong learnings that help me:

- Think better.
- Learn better.
- Communicate better.
- Write better.
- Express ideas more clearly.
- Make better decisions.
- Grow personally and professionally.
- Preserve meaningful and timeless knowledge.

It is NOT intended to become:

- A dictionary.
- A GRE/IELTS/TOEFL preparation repository.
- A collection of internet vocabulary lists.
- A temporary learning notebook.
- A dumping ground for random phrases, words, or expressions.
- A repository of impressive but impractical knowledge.

---

## Filter #0 - Permanent Storage Test

Before adding any new entry, ask yourself:

> Why does this deserve permanent storage?

Examples:

- It changed the way I think.
- It improved the way I communicate.
- It improved the way I learn.
- It has significant cultural or literary value.
- I frequently revisit this concept.
- I want to preserve this knowledge for life.

If you cannot clearly explain why it deserves permanent storage, it probably does not belong in this module.

---

## Inclusion Criteria

Before adding any new entry, ask the following questions.

### Filter #1 - Practical Usage

Will I realistically use or benefit from this in:

- Daily life?
- Communication?
- Professional work?
- Writing?
- Thinking and decision making?
- Personal growth?
- Lifelong learning?

Strongly prefer knowledge that has clear and lasting practical value.

---

### Filter #2 - Timelessness

Ask yourself:

> Will I be happy to rediscover this after 10, 20, or even 30 years?

Prefer:

- Timeless expressions.
- Professional vocabulary.
- Meaningful concepts.
- Valuable communication patterns.

Avoid:

- Temporary trends.
- Viral internet slang.
- Rarely used expressions.
- Buzzwords that will quickly become obsolete.

---

### Filter #3 - Lifetime Value

Prefer knowledge that improves one or more of the following:

- Thinking.
- Communication.
- Writing.
- Leadership discussions.
- Technical discussions.
- Documentation.
- Decision making.
- Critical thinking.
- Lifelong learning.
- Personal growth.

Examples:

- Due diligence.
- Pragmatic.
- The main takeaway is...
- From my perspective...
- Let's circle back.
- Skeptical.
- Curated.

---

### Filter #4 - Personal Value

Ask:

> Why am I storing this?

Acceptable reasons include:

- I frequently use it.
- I want to use it more naturally.
- It changed the way I think.
- It improves how I communicate.
- It has significant cultural or literary value.
- I want to preserve it for life.

---

### Filter #5 - Frequency of Usage

Prefer:

- Frequently used.
- Naturally spoken.
- Professionally useful.
- Easy to apply in real life.

Avoid:

- Extremely rare words.
- Overly academic vocabulary.
- Impressive but impractical expressions.

For example:

#### Prefer

- Curated
- Pragmatic
- Due diligence
- Skeptical
- The main takeaway is...
- If I understand you correctly...
- FOMO
- Shadowing

#### Avoid

- Top 5000 GRE words.
- Rare Shakespearean expressions.
- Difficult words that are never used naturally.
- Copy-pasted vocabulary lists.

---

### Filter #6 - Curated Knowledge Only

Never store:

- Dictionary dumps.
- Internet collections.
- AI-generated vocabulary lists.
- Temporary notes.
- Duplicate contents.
- Low-value examples.

Store only:

- Naturally acquired learnings.
- Personally meaningful knowledge.
- Frequently revisited concepts.
- High-value references.

---

### Filter #7 - Less Is Better

Prefer:

- 100 excellent words.

over

- 10,000 average words.

Prefer:

- 50 excellent expressions.

over

- 2,000 copied phrases.

Prefer:

- Highly curated knowledge.

over

- Large collections.

Quality always takes precedence over quantity.

---

## Golden Rules

Every entry should satisfy MOST of the following:

- Practical.
- Frequently useful.
- Professionally valuable.
- Timeless.
- Personally meaningful.
- Naturally learned.
- Worth preserving for a lifetime.
- Improves communication, thinking, or self-expression.

An entry does not need to satisfy every criterion. However, it should provide clear, lasting, and meaningful value worthy of preserving for a lifetime.
