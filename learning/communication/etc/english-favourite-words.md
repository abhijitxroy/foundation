# English Favourite Words


| Word | Meaning | When to Use | Example | Why Preserve It? |
|------|---------|-------------|---------|-----------------|
| Skeptical | Having reasonable doubts or questioning something. | Decision making | I'm skeptical about that claim. | Professional value |
| Stipulated | Clearly stated as a requirement or condition. | Documentation and contracts | The terms were stipulated in the agreement. | Professional value |
| Churn | Continuous change, turnover, or loss of customers/users. | Business discussions | The company is trying to reduce customer churn. | Frequently useful |
| Metaphor | A figure of speech that describes something by comparing it to something else. | Writing and speaking | He used a metaphor to explain the concept. | Lifetime value |
| Hypothetically | Based on assumptions rather than facts. | Discussions | Hypothetically, what would happen if the system failed? | Frequently useful |
| Lucrative | Producing significant profit or financial gain. | Career and finance discussions | It was a lucrative opportunity. | Frequently useful |
| Speculation | Forming opinions without complete information or evidence. | Discussions and investing | At this stage, it's only speculation. | Lifetime value |
| Prolong | To extend the duration of something. | Professional communication | Prolonged delays affect delivery schedules. | Frequently useful |
| Procrastination | Delaying necessary work unnecessarily. | Personal growth | Stop procrastinating and get started. | Lifetime value |
