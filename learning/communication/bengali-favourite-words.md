# Bengali

A curated collection of timeless Bengali words, expressions, and culturally significant terms that are worth preserving for lifelong learning.

## Inclusion Criteria

Only include entries that are:
- Timeless and culturally meaningful.
- Difficult to translate directly into English.
- Literary, memorable, or frequently worth revisiting.
- Personally or intellectually valuable.

Do not include dictionary-style word lists or temporary notes.

## Words & Expressions

| Word / Phrase | Meaning | Notes |
|---------------|---------|-------|
| **আকাশকুসুম (Akashkusum)** | An unrealistic dream or an impossible ambition. | Closest English expression: *"castles in the air"*. Often used in Bengali literature and conversation to describe fanciful or impractical aspirations. |
