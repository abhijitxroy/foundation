# Methodologies

> A highly curated collection of timeless methodologies, frameworks, and techniques that improve communication, learning, speaking, writing, and critical thinking.

## Inclusion Criteria

Only store methodologies that are:

- Practical and reusable.
- Professionally valuable.
- Timeless and worth revisiting.
- Helpful for improving communication or learning.
- Naturally acquired through meaningful experience and learning.

Do NOT store:

- Temporary challenges.
- Daily or weekly schedules.
- One-time practice plans.
- Low-value learning hacks.
- Copy-pasted internet collections.
