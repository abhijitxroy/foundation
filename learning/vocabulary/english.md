# English

A personal collection of useful English words and expressions that I want to remember, understand, and use naturally in conversation and writing.

## Words

| Word | Meaning / Note |
|------|----------------|
| **Zapardize** | To ruin or spoil something. |
| **Exhausted** | Extremely tired; completely drained of energy. |
| **Exaggerate** | To describe something as greater or more important than it really is. |
| **Breeze** | Something that is very easy to do; effortless. |

## Purpose

- Build a richer and more natural English vocabulary.
- Keep track of interesting words encountered while reading or listening.
- Maintain a personal reference for revision and everyday usage.
