# Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap describes the long-term direction of the **Foundation** repository and its role within the Personal Knowledge Ecosystem (PKE). It focuses on strategic milestones rather than day-to-day tasks.

> **Implementation tasks belong in `TODO.md` and the project backlog. This roadmap captures the long-term vision.**

---

# Vision

Build a structured, reusable, and evolving knowledge hub that captures, refines, and organizes knowledge across multiple domains while providing the methodologies, standards, and learning frameworks that support the entire Personal Knowledge Ecosystem.

---

# Strategic Objectives

- Build a lifelong knowledge repository.
- Develop reusable learning and research methodologies.
- Create consistent documentation and knowledge standards.
- Organize knowledge for long-term maintainability.
- Support continuous learning and future research projects.
- Serve as the governance and knowledge layer of the Personal Knowledge Ecosystem.

---

# Long-Term Milestones

## Phase 1 — Foundation

- Repository governance
- Repository standards
- Knowledge organization
- Core documentation

## Phase 2 — Knowledge

- Expand technology knowledge.
- Expand book notes and learning resources.
- Improve glossary, diagrams, and reference material.

## Phase 3 — Methodology

- Reading methodologies
- Research methodologies
- Documentation methodologies
- Knowledge management frameworks

## Phase 4 — Integration

- Strengthen integration with the `books` repository.
- Strengthen integration with the `india` repository.
- Improve cross-referencing and discoverability.

## Phase 5 — Evolution

- Knowledge graph exploration.
- Visual learning resources.
- Improved navigation and search.
- AI-assisted knowledge workflows.

---

# Personal Knowledge Ecosystem

Foundation is one part of the larger ecosystem:

- **foundation** — Knowledge, methodologies, standards, and reusable learning.
- **books** — Private digital library and source materials.
- **india** — Structured research and documentary knowledge.

Together they support the flow:

```text
Books
   ↓
Research (India)
   ↓
Knowledge (Foundation)
```

---

# Guiding Principle

The roadmap evolves over time as the repository matures, but its primary mission remains unchanged:

> Build a lifelong, organized, research-driven, and reusable knowledge foundation.
