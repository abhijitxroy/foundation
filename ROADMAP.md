# ROADMAP

## Purpose

This roadmap describes the long-term direction of the **Foundation** repository and its role within the Personal Knowledge Ecosystem (PKE). It focuses on strategic goals, repository growth, lifelong learning, and continuous knowledge refinement rather than day-to-day implementation tasks.

> **Implementation tasks belong in `TODO.md`. This roadmap captures the strategic and long-term vision of the repository.**

---

## Vision

Build a structured, reusable, and evolving lifelong knowledge ecosystem that captures, organizes, refines, and preserves knowledge across multiple domains while providing the methodologies, standards, and learning frameworks that support continuous growth.

Foundation is designed not merely as a collection of notes, but as a practical and reusable engineering and learning system.

---

## Strategic Objectives

The primary objectives of Foundation are to:

- Build a lifelong knowledge repository.
- Develop reusable learning and research methodologies.
- Preserve practical engineering knowledge.
- Create structured learning roadmaps across domains.
- Establish consistent documentation and knowledge standards.
- Support continuous learning and interdisciplinary exploration.
- Develop system-level engineering thinking.
- Promote technical excellence and lifelong growth.
- Serve as the knowledge and learning layer of the Personal Knowledge Ecosystem.
