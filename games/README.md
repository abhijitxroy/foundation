# Games

This section covers the fundamentals of three of the world's most popular and influential games.

* **Football** – The world's most popular team sport, played and watched in almost every country.
* **Cricket** – One of the most followed sports, especially in Asia, Australia, England, and several other nations.
* **Chess** – A classic strategy game that develops logical thinking, planning, and problem-solving skills.

The focus is on understanding the basics of each game, including the playing area, player positions, rules, formats, major tournaments, records, and commonly used terms.
