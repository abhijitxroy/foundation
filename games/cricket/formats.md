# Formats

Cricket is played in three main international formats. Each format differs in the number of overs, match duration, and playing style.

---

## Test Cricket

* Longest format of the game.
* Played over **5 days**.
* Each team gets **2 innings**.
* No limit on the number of overs.
* Considered the highest test of skill, patience, and endurance.

---

## One Day International (ODI)

* Each team bats for **50 overs**.
* Usually completed in **one day**.
* Introduced to make the game shorter and more entertaining.
* Used for the **ICC Cricket World Cup**.

---

## Twenty20 International (T20I)

* Each team bats for **20 overs**.
* Usually lasts about **3 to 4 hours**.
* Fast-paced and aggressive format.
* Used for the **ICC T20 World Cup**.

---

## Comparison

| Feature          | Test                           | ODI     | T20I      |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------ | ------- | --------- |
| Innings per Team | 2                              | 1       | 1         |
| Overs per Team   | Unlimited                      | 50      | 20        |
| Match Duration   | Up to 5 Days                   | 1 Day   | 3–4 Hours |
| Clothing         | White                          | Colored | Colored   |
| Ball             | Red (Pink for Day-Night Tests) | White   | White     |

---

## Major ICC Tournaments

| Tournament                  | Format |
| --------------------------- | ------ |
| ICC Cricket World Cup       | ODI    |
| ICC T20 World Cup           | T20I   |
| ICC World Test Championship | Test   |
| ICC Champions Trophy        | ODI    |
