# Football

Football is the world's most popular sport, played professionally and recreationally in almost every country. It is a team sport played between two teams of **11 players** each, where the objective is to score more goals than the opponent within the allotted time.

Football is known for its speed, teamwork, skill, strategy, and global fan base. The **FIFA World Cup** is the most prestigious football tournament in the world.

---

## Overview

* **Type:** Outdoor Team Sport
* **Players:** 11 vs 11
* **Match Duration:** 90 minutes (45 + 45)
* **Objective:** Score more goals than the opponent.
* **Governing Body:** FIFA
