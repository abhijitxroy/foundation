# Openings

A **chess opening** is the first phase of the game, usually covering the first **8–15 moves**. A good opening helps develop your pieces, control the center of the board, and prepare for the middle game.

---

## Opening Principles

Follow these basic principles in every game:

* Control the center squares.
* Develop knights and bishops early.
* Castle your king to safety.
* Avoid moving the same piece repeatedly.
* Do not bring the queen out too early.

---

## Common Chess Openings

| Opening               | First Moves                | Description                                        |
| --------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Italian Game          | 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bc4 | Simple opening that develops pieces quickly.       |
| Ruy Lopez             | 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bb5 | One of the oldest and most popular openings.       |
| Sicilian Defense      | 1. e4 c5                   | Aggressive defense often used by Black.            |
| French Defense        | 1. e4 e6                   | Solid defensive opening for Black.                 |
| Queen's Gambit        | 1. d4 d5 2. c4             | Popular opening focused on controlling the center. |
| King's Indian Defense | 1. d4 Nf6                  | Flexible and strategic defense for Black.          |

---

## Which Opening Should Beginners Learn?

If you're new to chess, these openings are easy to understand:

* Italian Game
* Ruy Lopez
* Queen's Gambit

Focus on learning the opening principles rather than memorizing long move sequences.

---

## Interesting Facts

* There are hundreds of recognized chess openings.
* Every opening has a unique code in the **Encyclopaedia of Chess Openings (ECO)**.
* Professional players often spend years studying opening theory.
* A strong opening provides a good position but does not guarantee victory.
